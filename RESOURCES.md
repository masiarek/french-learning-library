# Resources

**Level:** reference · for choosing what to read next

**One line:** The dictionaries and books behind the pages, with what each is good for.

| Resource | Good for | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| Oxford Learner's Dictionaries, [oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com ↗](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/) | British and American IPA for every English word, with audio; the English side of any comparison | [English and French in the IPA](01_Sounds/ipa_english_vs_french/README.md) |
| Le Robert, *Dictionnaire de la langue française* | The standard French pronunciation key, with the four nasal vowels | [English and French in the IPA](01_Sounds/ipa_english_vs_french/README.md) |
| Assimil, *French with Ease* | A course whose first lessons give every sentence in IPA; the owner's course | the sentences to transcribe |
| International Phonetic Association, *Handbook of the IPA* (Cambridge, 1999) | The alphabet itself, with an illustration per language; Jassem's Polish illustration is the Polish key | [Four inventories](01_Sounds/four_inventories/README.md) |
| Duden, *Das Aussprachewörterbuch* (7th ed., 2015) | The standard German pronunciation key | [Four inventories](01_Sounds/four_inventories/README.md) |
| Yosser, the owner's French teacher: the worksheets of the course | A reading text, exercises, a grammar point and a Corrigé per lesson | [Yosser (teacher)](02_Yosser_teacher/README.md) |
| Szypowska, a Polish textbook of French (the title is not on the pages in hand) | Numbered lessons, each with a dialogue in the book's own phonetic notation, a word list with Polish glosses, grammar in Polish and exercises | [Szypowska (Polish material)](03_Szypowska/README.md) |
