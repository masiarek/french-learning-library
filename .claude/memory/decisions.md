# Decisions

Dated log of decisions and preferences, with the reason. Newest first.

- 2026-10-10 — **The French alphabet is a Sounds lesson**, `01_Sounds/french_alphabet`, not a source page. The owner sent French Wikipedia's table of the 26 letters, asked whether it was IPA, then asked for an alphabet page. Its argument is that letter names are French words that obey French sound rules, which a program checks: every *e* closed at the end and open before a consonant (16 of 16), the vowel-after or vowel-before shape of a consonant's name predicted by the letter's Latin class (14 of 14) and not by its French sound (*c, g, j, v* break it), and *ache* the one name without its letter's sound. The names follow Le Robert, [a] for *a* and *ka* where Wikipedia writes /ɑ/, and the how-sure table says so. The words for spelling aloud (*accent aigu, cédille, tréma, e dans l'o*) are on the same page rather than a page of their own, because they are a table and not an argument.
- 2026-10-10 — **A word page goes up without its screenshot** when the owner sends the word alone (“add page to 1000 words : une chaise”): the entry row and the example are written after the app's pattern, the chapter page says so, and the inbox holds the swap for when the screenshot comes, as the Pronunciation chapter did for its first video.
- 2026-10-09 — **The vocabulary app's words are a chapter of their own, one page per word**, `05_1000_words`. The owner first asked for a folder with the entries, IPA and my examples at the bottom, then, six words later, for separate pages with grammar explanations and helpful phrases; so each word is a lesson folder with the app's entry as its table, an AI section of grammar, set phrases, examples and exercises, and two Anki decks in sets of their own, 1000 words (FR-EN) and (EN-FR). The sidebar sorts the pages A to Z, so `NAV_ORDER` lists only the chapter page, and the chapter page numbers the words in order of arrival, so the last number is the count. Folder names drop accents (`etre`), as `lecon_04_chez_les_lefevre` does. Set names with a space become one Anki tag (`1000words`), by a change to `anki_from_tables.py`.
- 2026-10-09 — **The pronunciation resources guide lives inside the Pronunciation chapter**, `04_Pronunciation/resources`, because the owner asked for “a page re best pronunciation resources” right after “under pronunciation”, and reads the chapter on a phone. Its lists are four-column tables (resource, what it gives, why useful, how sure), which `anki_from_tables.py` and `check_ipa.py` ignore by design, and it ends with a three-column table of the French words of pronunciation, which does make cards. A page whose screenshot has not arrived carries the drill alone and says so, rather than wait: the first page, on /ɑ̃/ and /ɔ̃/, went up that way, the second, on /ɛ/, from a screenshot.
