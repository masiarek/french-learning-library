Greeting or goodbye: bonsoir, bonne soirée?	Bonsoir /bɔ̃swaʁ/ greets; bonne soirée /bɔn swaʁe/ says goodbye.	greetings
Why not “bon après-midi, tout le monde” on arriving?	It only says goodbye. On arriving: Bonjour, tout le monde ! /bɔ̃ʒuʁ, tu lə mɔ̃d/	greetings
How do you pronounce à plus (see you)?	/a plys/: the s is heard. À plus tard: /a ply taʁ/	pronunciation
You walk into a bakery. First word?	Bonjour, madame ! /bɔ̃ʒuʁ, madam/ — always before the question.	greetings
Say in French: See you tomorrow! See you soon!	À demain ! À bientôt ! /a dəmɛ̃, a bjɛ̃to/	goodbyes
Is “bon matin” French?	Not in France (say bonjour /bɔ̃ʒuʁ/); in Québec it is a normal greeting.	greetings
Who gets coucou, salut, bonjour?	Coucou /kuku/: family, close friends. Salut /saly/: friends. Bonjour /bɔ̃ʒuʁ/: everyone else.	register
