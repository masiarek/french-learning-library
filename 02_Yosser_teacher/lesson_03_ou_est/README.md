## AI section: new contexts, new words, new exercises

Mine, not the source's. The audio, made on the owner's Mac with the [recipe in Resources](../../RESOURCES.md#audio-of-a-lesson-on-a-mac), reads the table above and then these exercises, with a pause before each answer.

- **Où est** /u ɛ/ for one thing, **où sont** /u sɔ̃/ for several: *où sont les toilettes ?* /u sɔ̃ le twalɛt/. *Toilettes* /twalɛt/ is plural in French, so it takes *sont*.
- **Où se trouve** /u sə tʁuv/ is the same question, a little more formal, and what a map or a guidebook says: *le musée se trouve en face de la gare* /lə myze sə tʁuv ɑ̃ fas də la ɡaʁ/.
- **Quel** /kɛl/ agrees with the noun even when *être* stands between them: *quelle est la station ?* /kɛl ɛ la stasjɔ̃/ is feminine because *la station* /la stasjɔ̃/ is. Before a vowel the plural links: *quels hôtels ?* /kɛlz‿otɛl/, the one place where the four forms do not sound the same.
- **Qu’est-ce que** /kɛs kə/ asks *what*, **est-ce que** /ɛs kə/ asks *yes or no*: *est-ce que le musée est loin ?* /ɛs kə lə myze ɛ lwɛ̃/. The source's own heading “Common mistakes” says “nothing to mention”; this pair is the one I would mention.
- The [*être*](../lesson_02_etre_avoir/README.md) /ɛtʁ/ of lesson 2 and the prepositions of place of [lesson 1](../lesson_01_la_maison/README.md), *devant, derrière, à côté de* /dəvɑ̃, dɛʁjɛʁ, a kote də/, are the answers to these questions.
