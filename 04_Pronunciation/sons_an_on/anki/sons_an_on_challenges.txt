Which spellings give /ɑ̃/, and which /ɔ̃/?	an, am, en, em → /ɑ̃/ (blanc, chambre, enfant, temps); on, om → /ɔ̃/ (bon, nom, ombre); the m spellings come before b and p	sound
Why is bonne /bɔn/ when bon is /bɔ̃/?	A doubled n or m, or a vowel after, undoes the nasal: bon /bɔ̃/, bonne /bɔn/, un bon ami /œ̃ bɔn‿ami/; the same for an /ɑ̃/ and année /ane/	sound
