# 06_Expressions — phrases, one page each

**Level:** 101 · for the owner, one phrase at a time

Whole phrases the owner wants to say, kept one page each: the phrase as the owner sent it, with Le Robert's IPA on every line, and under it an AI section with the grammar of the phrase, the ways to vary it, the trap that sounds like it, set phrases on the same word, my own examples and exercises with the answers, every line in French, English and IPA. A single word goes to [1000 words](../05_1000_words/README.md); a phrase goes here, and its key word gets a page there too.

The table numbers the phrases in the order they arrived. The sidebar lists the pages A to Z.

| # | Expression | English | On the page |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [J'ai appris la leçon par cœur.](par_coeur/README.md) /ʒe apʁi la ləsɔ̃ paʁ kœʁ/ | I memorized the lesson; I know it by heart | *apprendre, savoir, connaître par cœur*, *j'ai mémorisé*, the trap *j'ai retenu la leçon* (I learned my lesson), *apprendre* and *connaître* in the present, *cœur* and *chœur*, set phrases with *cœur* |

How to read a page: the phrase first, then the table, French, English, IPA after Le Robert's key, from memory. Bold rows name the sections, `________` is a blank, ‿ a compulsory liaison, a · separates the two words of a minimal pair, and the answer to a blank stands in square brackets in the IPA.

## Anki

Two sets, kept apart from the other chapters', each one file with a subdeck per phrase:

| Set | File | On the front | On the back |
|---|---|---|---|
| Expressions (FR-EN) | [`anki/expressions_fr_en.txt`](anki/expressions_fr_en.txt) | the French, with the IPA under it | the English; also the exercises, with their answers |
| Expressions (EN-FR) | [`anki/expressions_en_fr.txt`](anki/expressions_en_fr.txt) | the English | the French and the IPA; also the challenges written by hand, under their own subdeck |

Each phrase folder holds its own decks under `anki/`: the two generated from the page's tables by `tools/anki_from_tables.py`, the challenges, and two import files, `<phrase>_import_expressions_fr_en.txt` and `<phrase>_import_expressions_en_fr.txt`, for importing a new phrase on its own.
