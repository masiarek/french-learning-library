# par cœur — by heart

## The phrase

Conventions: on the [chapter page](../README.md).

- *J'ai appris la leçon par cœur.* /ʒe apʁi la ləsɔ̃ paʁ kœʁ/ is the usual way to say *I memorized the lesson*: *apprendre* /apʁɑ̃dʁ/ to learn, in the passé composé, *j'ai appris* /ʒe apʁi/, with the final *s* of *appris* silent.
- *Je connais la leçon par cœur.* /ʒə kɔnɛ la ləsɔ̃ paʁ kœʁ/ is *I know it by heart*: the state after the learning, with *connaître* /kɔnɛtʁ/ in the present.
- *Par cœur* /paʁ kœʁ/ is word for word *by heart*, the same picture as English: *cœur* /kœʁ/ heart, written with the *œ* ligature and said like *sœur* /sœʁ/ sister.
- *La leçon* /la ləsɔ̃/ is feminine, and its *ç* is /s/ before *o*: *une leçon* /yn ləsɔ̃/, *les leçons* /le ləsɔ̃/.

## AI section: grammar, phrases, examples, exercises

- **Three verbs, one phrase.** *Apprendre par cœur* /apʁɑ̃dʁ paʁ kœʁ/ is the learning, *savoir par cœur* /savwaʁ paʁ kœʁ/ and *connaître par cœur* /kɔnɛtʁ paʁ kœʁ/ the knowing, *réciter par cœur* /ʁesite paʁ kœʁ/ the saying aloud. *Je sais ma leçon* /ʒə sɛ ma ləsɔ̃/ is what a pupil says to the teacher; *je connais* is as common with *par cœur*, and the only one for a person or a place: *je connais Paris par cœur* /ʒə kɔnɛ paʁi paʁ kœʁ/ I know Paris inside out.
- ***Mémoriser* is correct and plainer.** *J'ai mémorisé la leçon* /ʒe memɔʁize la ləsɔ̃/ says the same but sounds like a textbook; *par cœur* is what people say.
- **The trap: *retenir la leçon*.** *J'ai retenu la leçon* /ʒe ʁətəny la ləsɔ̃/ most often means *I learned my lesson*, from a mistake, like the English trap *I learned my lesson*. In class it can also mean *I remembered the lesson*; the context decides. *Ça me servira de leçon* /sa mə sɛʁviʁa də ləsɔ̃/ is *that will teach me*.
- ***Apprendre* and *connaître* in the present.** *Apprendre* goes like *prendre*: *j'apprends, nous apprenons, ils apprennent* /ʒapʁɑ̃, nuz‿apʁənɔ̃, ilz‿apʁɛn/, and the past participle *appris* /apʁi/. *Connaître* keeps *-aiss-* in the plural: *je connais, nous connaissons, ils connaissent* /ʒə kɔnɛ, nu kɔnɛsɔ̃, il kɔnɛs/, and *il connaît* /il kɔnɛ/ with its circumflex, which the 1990 spelling reform lets go, *il connait*.
- ***Cœur* and *chœur*.** *Le cœur* the heart and *le chœur* /lə kœʁ/ the choir are homophones; *en chœur* /ɑ̃ kœʁ/ is *in chorus*, *par cœur* is *by heart*. The *œ* also spells /œ/ in *la sœur, un œuf* /la sœʁ, œ̃n‿œf/ and /ø/ in *des œufs* /dez‿ø/.
