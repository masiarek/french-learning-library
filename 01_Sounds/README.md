# 01_Sounds — what does the French in the dictionary sound like?

**Level:** 101 · for anyone starting French from a book

French spelling hides its sounds: *eau, au, o* and *ô* are one vowel, *vin, vingt, vain* and *vint* are one word to the ear, and the last letters of *petit* are silent until a vowel follows. Every French dictionary therefore gives each word in the International Phonetic Alphabet, between slashes, and so does the first lesson of every course book. This chapter is about reading that alphabet. It opens with what an English reader already knows of it and what is new, and like every chapter here it backs each claim with a program that runs.
