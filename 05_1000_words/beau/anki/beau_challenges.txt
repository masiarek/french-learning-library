The five forms of beau, with IPA	beau /bo/, bel /bɛl/, belle /bɛl/, beaux /bo/, belles /bɛl/: five spellings, two sounds	grammar
When is the masculine bel used?	Before a masculine noun starting with a vowel or a mute h, in the singular: un bel appartement /œ̃ bɛl apaʁtəmɑ̃/, un bel homme /œ̃ bɛl ɔm/; after être it is beau: cet homme est beau /sɛt‿ɔm ɛ bo/	grammar
Beautiful eyes: why de and not des, and what is the liaison?	Before a plural adjective that precedes its noun des becomes de, and the x is said as /z/: de beaux yeux /də boz‿jø/	grammar
Beau or joli: a handsome man, fine weather, a pretty village	un bel homme /œ̃ bɛl ɔm/, il fait beau /il fɛ bo/, un joli village /œ̃ ʒɔli vilaʒ/: beau for men and weather, joli for the small and charming	words
Mother-in-law, father-in-law, brother-in-law, sister-in-law	la belle-mère /la bɛlmɛʁ/, le beau-père /lə bopɛʁ/, le beau-frère /lə bofʁɛʁ/, la belle-sœur /la bɛlsœʁ/	words
A beautiful girl and a daughter-in-law: what tells them apart?	The hyphen: une belle fille /yn bɛl fij/ is beautiful, une belle-fille /yn bɛlfij/ is a daughter-in-law	words
Read aloud, then check: beau, bon, bout, bu	/bo/ beautiful, /bɔ̃/ good, /bu/ end, /by/ drunk: four vowels on one consonant	sound
