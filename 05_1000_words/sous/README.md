# sous — under

## The word

Conventions: on the [chapter page](../README.md).

- *sous* /su/: the vowel is /u/, the *ou* of *tout* /tu/ and *vous* /vu/, lips rounded and tongue back. Its neighbour is [sur](../sur/README.md) /syʁ/ with /y/, lips rounded and tongue forward; say *sous, sur* /su, syʁ/ one after the other and only the tongue moves. The *-s* is silent before a consonant, *sous la table* /su la tabl/, and said as /z/ before a vowel, *sous un arbre* /suz‿œ̃n‿aʁbʁ/, *sous une chaise* /suz‿yn ʃɛz/.
- Three words sound the same: *sous* /su/ under, *un sou* /œ̃ su/ a penny, as in *je n'ai pas un sou* /ʒə ne pa œ̃ su/ I haven't a penny, and *saoul* or *soûl* /su/ drunk. Against them *su* /sy/ known, the participle of *savoir* /savwaʁ/, is the minimal pair /u/ and /y/.
- *Sous* is under or beneath, *sous la table* /su la tabl/, *sous le lit* /su lə li/; and it is the French *in* for what comes down from above, *sous la pluie* /su la plɥi/ in the rain, *sous le soleil* /su lə sɔlɛj/ in the sun, *sous la douche* /su la duʃ/ in the shower.
- With no noun after it the word is *dessous* /dəsu/ underneath, and the family runs on: *au-dessous de* /odəsu də/ below, *là-dessous* /ladəsu/ under there, *le dessous* /lə dəsu/ the underside. The compounds in *sous-* are everyday words, *le sous-sol* /lə susɔl/ the basement, *les sous-titres* /le sutitʁ/ the subtitles.
- It is the third key word of section 9 of the teacher's [La maison](../../02_Yosser_teacher/lesson_01_la_maison/README.md): *Le sac est sous la table.* /lə sak ɛ su la tabl/.

## AI section: grammar, phrases, examples, exercises

- **Sous, sur, dans.** Under, on, in: *sous la table* /su la tabl/, *sur la table* /syʁ la tabl/, [dans](../dans/README.md) *la boîte* /dɑ̃ la bwat/. *Sous* is also below in a ranking or a measure: *sous zéro* /su zeʁo/ below zero, *sous la moyenne* /su la mwajɛn/ below average; and *au-dessous de* /odəsu də/ says the same with a noun, *au-dessous de la fenêtre* /odəsu də la fənɛtʁ/ below the window.
- **Sous where English says in.** What falls or shines from above: *sous la pluie* /su la plɥi/ in the rain, *sous la neige* /su la nɛʒ/ in the snow, *sous le soleil* /su lə sɔlɛj/ in the sun, *sous la douche* /su la duʃ/ in the shower; and what covers: *sous terre* /su tɛʁ/ underground, *sous l'eau* /su lo/ under water, *sous clé* /su kle/ under lock and key, *sous enveloppe* /suz‿ɑ̃vlɔp/ in an envelope.
- **Sous in time and rule.** *Sous peu* /su pø/ shortly, *sous huit jours* /su ɥi ʒuʁ/ within a week, *sous Louis XIV* /su lwi katɔʁz/ in the reign of Louis XIV, *sous la direction de* /su la diʁɛksjɔ̃ də/ under the direction of, *sous pression* /su pʁɛsjɔ̃/ under pressure, *sous forme de* /su fɔʁm də/ in the form of, *sous le nom de* /su lə nɔ̃ də/ under the name of.
- **Dessous and its family.** *Dessous* /dəsu/ is the adverb, *le chat est dessous* /lə ʃa ɛ dəsu/ the cat is underneath; *en dessous* /ɑ̃ dəsu/ underneath, below; *là-dessous* /ladəsu/ under there, and figuratively *il y a quelque chose là-dessous* /il j a kɛlkə ʃoz ladəsu/ there is something behind this; *le dessous* /lə dəsu/ the underside and *les dessous* /le dəsu/ underwear; *sens dessus dessous* /sɑ̃ dəsy dəsu/ upside down, in chaos, with *dessus* /dəsy/ on top, the mirror word of [sur](../sur/README.md).
- **The words in sous-.** *Le sous-sol* /lə susɔl/ the basement, *un sous-marin* /œ̃ sumaʁɛ̃/ a submarine, *les sous-titres* /le sutitʁ/ the subtitles, *un sous-vêtement* /œ̃ suvɛtmɑ̃/ an undergarment, *un sous-entendu* /œ̃ suzɑ̃tɑ̃dy/ an insinuation, *souligner* /suliɲe/ to underline, *soutenir* /sutniʁ/ to hold up, to support, *soumettre* /sumɛtʁ/ to submit: *sous* is the French *sub-* and *under-*.
