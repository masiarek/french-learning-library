# une fenêtre — a window

## The word

Conventions: on the [chapter page](../README.md).

- *une fenêtre* /yn fənɛtʁ/: three syllables on paper, two in the mouth, the first *e* a light /ə/ that fast speech drops, /fnɛtʁ/. The circumflex *ê* is the open /ɛ/ and remembers a lost *s*: Latin *fenestra*, English *defenestration*. The ending *-tre* is the cluster /tʁ/ with no vowel after it, as in *être* /ɛtʁ/, *quatre* /katʁ/, *notre* /nɔtʁ/.
- *Une* /yn/ says the noun is feminine: *la fenêtre* /la fənɛtʁ/, *les fenêtres* /le fənɛtʁ/.
- *Par la fenêtre* /paʁ la fənɛtʁ/ is through the window, out of the window: *regarder par la fenêtre* /ʁəɡaʁde paʁ la fənɛtʁ/ to look out of the window, and the idiom *jeter l'argent par les fenêtres* /ʒəte laʁʒɑ̃ paʁ le fənɛtʁ/ to throw money away.
- *Donner sur* /dɔne syʁ/ is what a window does: *la fenêtre donne sur le jardin* /la fənɛtʁ dɔn syʁ lə ʒaʁdɛ̃/ the window looks onto the garden, one more sense of [sur](../sur/README.md).

## AI section: grammar, phrases, examples, exercises

- **Ouvrir and fermer.** *Ouvrir* /uvʁiʁ/ is irregular but takes the endings of an *-er* verb in the present: *j'ouvre* /ʒuvʁ/, *tu ouvres* /ty uvʁ/, *il ouvre* /il uvʁ/, *nous ouvrons* /nuz‿uvʁɔ̃/, *vous ouvrez* /vuz‿uvʁe/, *ils ouvrent* /ilz‿uvʁ/; past participle *ouvert* /uvɛʁ/, feminine *ouverte* /uvɛʁt/, where the *t* comes to be said. *Fermer* /fɛʁme/ is regular: *je ferme* /ʒə fɛʁm/, *nous fermons* /nu fɛʁmɔ̃/, *fermé* /fɛʁme/. The state agrees with the noun: *la fenêtre est ouverte* /la fənɛtʁ ɛt‿uvɛʁt/, *la porte est fermée* /la pɔʁt ɛ fɛʁme/.
- **The parts of the window.** *La vitre* /la vitʁ/ the pane, also *le carreau* /lə kaʁo/, which is a floor tile too; *le volet* /lə vɔlɛ/ the shutter; *le rideau* /lə ʁido/ the curtain, *les rideaux* /le ʁido/; *le store* /lə stɔʁ/ the blind; *le rebord de la fenêtre* /lə ʁəbɔʁ də la fənɛtʁ/ the window sill; *la porte-fenêtre* /la pɔʁtfənɛtʁ/ the French window; *la vitrine* /la vitʁin/ the shop window.
- **The window and the weather.** *Il fait froid, ferme la fenêtre* /il fɛ fʁwa, fɛʁm la fənɛtʁ/; *il fait chaud, ouvre la fenêtre* /il fɛ ʃo, uvʁ la fənɛtʁ/; *il y a un courant d'air* /il j a œ̃ kuʁɑ̃ dɛʁ/ there is a draught; *aérer la pièce* /aeʁe la pjɛs/ to air the room.
- **On the train and the plane.** *Une place côté fenêtre* /yn plas kote fənɛtʁ/ a window seat, *côté couloir* /kote kulwaʁ/ an aisle seat; the window of a plane or a ship is *le hublot* /lə yblo/, with an *h* that blocks elision and liaison, *le hublot*, not *l'hublot*.
- **On the screen.** *Une fenêtre* is a window on a computer as well: *ouvrir une nouvelle fenêtre* /uvʁiʁ yn nuvɛl fənɛtʁ/, *fermer la fenêtre* /fɛʁme la fənɛtʁ/.
