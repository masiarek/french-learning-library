The forms of confortable, with IPA	confortable /kɔ̃fɔʁtabl/ for both genders, confortables /kɔ̃fɔʁtabl/ in the plural: an adjective already ending in -e adds no feminine -e, and the four are said alike	grammar
Where does confortable go, before or after the noun?	After, as most adjectives do: une maison confortable /yn mɛzɔ̃ kɔ̃fɔʁtabl/; with a short adjective before it, une grande maison confortable /yn ɡʁɑ̃d mɛzɔ̃ kɔ̃fɔʁtabl/	grammar
Can a person be confortable?	No: a chair, a bed, a house or a salary is confortable; a person is bien /bjɛ̃/, à l’aise /a lɛz/ or bien installé /bjɛ̃n‿ɛ̃stale/; je suis confortable is a Quebec anglicism	grammar
