The three sounds that spell maison	ai as the open /ɛ/, s between vowels as /z/, -on as the nasal /ɔ̃/: /mɛzɔ̃/, rhyming with raison and saison	sound
I live, we live, they live	j’habite /ʒabit/, nous habitons /nuz‿abitɔ̃/, ils habitent /ilz‿abit/: the h is mute, so elision and liaison apply	grammar
