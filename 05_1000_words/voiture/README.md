# une voiture — a car

## The word

Conventions: on the [chapter page](../README.md).

- *une voiture* /yn vwatyʁ/: *oi* is /wa/, as in *moi* /mwa/, *trois* /tʁwa/ and *l'armoire* /laʁmwaʁ/ on the page of [une armoire](../armoire/README.md); *u* is the French /y/ of [sur](../sur/README.md), the lips rounded as for /u/ and the tongue forward as for /i/; the final *-e* is silent and the *r* is said. So *voiture* rhymes with *la toiture* /la twatyʁ/ the roof, *la peinture* /la pɛ̃tyʁ/ the paint and *la nature* /la natyʁ/.
- *Une* /yn/ says the noun is feminine: *la voiture* /la vwatyʁ/, *les voitures* /le vwatyʁ/, *des voitures* /de vwatyʁ/, *ma voiture* /ma vwatyʁ/, the plural *-s* silent as always. Nouns in *-ure* are feminine: *la voiture, la toiture, la peinture, la nature, la confiture* /la kɔ̃fityʁ/ the jam.
- The teacher's first lesson, [La maison](../../02_Yosser_teacher/lesson_01_la_maison/README.md), has it under the prepositions of place, *La voiture est devant la maison* /la vwatyʁ ɛ dəvɑ̃ la mɛzɔ̃/, with *devant* /dəvɑ̃/ in front of against *derrière* /dɛʁjɛʁ/ behind, and *le garage* /lə ɡaʁaʒ/ among the rooms of the house, where one puts it: *garer la voiture* /ɡaʁe la vwatyʁ/ to park the car.
- *Une voiture* is the everyday word. *Une auto* /yn oto/, short for *une automobile* /yn otɔmɔbil/, is older and lives on in compounds, *une auto-école* /yn otoekɔl/ a driving school, *l'autoroute* /lotɔʁut/ the motorway; *un véhicule* /œ̃ veikyl/ is the word of the road signs and the insurance; *une bagnole* /yn baɲɔl/ is the slang. On the railway *une voiture* is a carriage: *voiture 12, place 45* /vwatyʁ duz, plas kaʁɑ̃t sɛ̃k/ on the ticket.
- *En voiture* /ɑ̃ vwatyʁ/ is *by car*, with no article: *aller au travail en voiture* /ale o tʁavaj ɑ̃ vwatyʁ/ to drive to work, like *en train* /ɑ̃ tʁɛ̃/, *en bus* /ɑ̃ bys/, *en avion* /ɑ̃n‿avjɔ̃/, for whatever one sits inside, against *à pied* /a pje/ on foot and *à vélo* /a velo/ by bike. *En voiture !* /ɑ̃ vwatyʁ/ is the call on the platform, *all aboard*.

## AI section: grammar, phrases, examples, exercises

- **Conduire, to drive.** An irregular verb with /ɥ/, the glide of *huit* /ɥit/ and *la cuisine* /la kɥizin/: *je conduis* /ʒə kɔ̃dɥi/, *tu conduis* /ty kɔ̃dɥi/, *il conduit* /il kɔ̃dɥi/, *nous conduisons* /nu kɔ̃dɥizɔ̃/, *vous conduisez* /vu kɔ̃dɥize/, *ils conduisent* /il kɔ̃dɥiz/; past participle *conduit* /kɔ̃dɥi/, *j'ai conduit toute la nuit* /ʒe kɔ̃dɥi tut la nɥi/ I drove all night. The three singular forms sound alike, and the plural adds /z/. *Conduire* is also to take someone somewhere: *conduire les enfants à l'école* /kɔ̃dɥiʁ lez‿ɑ̃fɑ̃ a lekɔl/ to drive the children to school. *Le permis de conduire* /lə pɛʁmi də kɔ̃dɥiʁ/ is the driving licence, *passer son permis* /pase sɔ̃ pɛʁmi/ to take one's test, *avoir le permis* /avwaʁ lə pɛʁmi/ to have a licence.
- **Garer, se garer.** *Garer la voiture* /ɡaʁe la vwatyʁ/ to park the car, and the reflexive *se garer* /sə ɡaʁe/ for the driver: *je me gare devant la maison* /ʒə mə ɡaʁ dəvɑ̃ la mɛzɔ̃/ I park in front of the house. *Une place de stationnement* /yn plas də stasjɔnmɑ̃/ is a parking space, *stationnement interdit* /stasjɔnmɑ̃ ɛ̃tɛʁdi/ the sign that says no parking, and *stationner* /stasjɔne/ the official verb for *garer*.
- **The verbs of the car.** *rouler* /ʁule/ to drive along, to go, of a car: *la voiture roule vite* /la vwatyʁ ʁul vit/, *rouler à cent trente* /ʁule a sɑ̃ tʁɑ̃t/ to do 130; *démarrer* /demaʁe/ to start, *la voiture ne démarre pas* /la vwatyʁ nə demaʁ pa/ the car won't start; *freiner* /fʁɛne/ to brake, *accélérer* /akseleʁe/ to speed up; *monter dans la voiture* /mɔ̃te dɑ̃ la vwatyʁ/ to get in, *descendre de la voiture* /desɑ̃dʁ də la vwatyʁ/ to get out; *louer une voiture* /lwe yn vwatyʁ/ to hire a car; *laver la voiture* /lave la vwatyʁ/; *tomber en panne* /tɔ̃be ɑ̃ pan/ to break down; *faire le plein* /fɛʁ lə plɛ̃/ to fill up; *prendre la voiture* /pʁɑ̃dʁ la vwatyʁ/ to take the car.
- **The car's parts.** *le volant* /lə vɔlɑ̃/ the steering wheel, *le siège* /lə sjɛʒ/ the seat, the same word as on the page of [une chaise](../chaise/README.md), *la ceinture de sécurité* /la sɛ̃tyʁ də sekyʁite/ the seatbelt, *le moteur* /lə mɔtœʁ/ the engine, *une roue* /yn ʁu/ a wheel, *un pneu* /œ̃ pnø/ a tyre, *le coffre* /lə kɔfʁ/ the boot, *la portière* /la pɔʁtjɛʁ/ the car door, which is not *la porte* of the page of [une porte](../porte/README.md), *le pare-brise* /lə paʁbʁiz/ the windscreen, *les phares* /le faʁ/ the headlights, *le klaxon* /lə klaksɔn/ the horn, *l'essence* /lesɑ̃s/ petrol, *les clés de la voiture* /le kle də la vwatyʁ/.
- **Kinds of car.** *une voiture neuve* /yn vwatyʁ nœv/ a new car, where *neuf, neuve* /nœf, nœv/ means brand new and *nouveau* /nuvo/ new to its owner; *une voiture d'occasion* /yn vwatyʁ dɔkazjɔ̃/ a second-hand car; *une voiture de location* /yn vwatyʁ də lɔkasjɔ̃/ a hire car; *une voiture de sport* /yn vwatyʁ də spɔʁ/; *une voiture électrique* /yn vwatyʁ elɛktʁik/; *une voiture de course* /yn vwatyʁ də kuʁs/ a racing car; *une voiture de police* /yn vwatyʁ də pɔlis/; *une voiture de fonction* /yn vwatyʁ də fɔ̃ksjɔ̃/ a company car; *une petite voiture, une grosse voiture* /yn pətit vwatyʁ, yn ɡʁos vwatyʁ/. *Une voiture d'enfant* /yn vwatyʁ dɑ̃fɑ̃/ is an old word for a pram, now *une poussette* /yn pusɛt/.
- **Voiture and toiture.** *La voiture* /la vwatyʁ/ and *la toiture* /la twatyʁ/ the roof differ by the first consonant alone, /v/ against /t/. *Un voiturier* /œ̃ vwatyʁje/ is the valet who parks the cars at a hotel. And *En voiture, Simone !* /ɑ̃ vwatyʁ, simɔn/ is a catchphrase for *let's go!*, from the name of a racing driver of the 1930s.
