# le cœur — the heart

## The word

Conventions: on the [chapter page](../README.md).

- *le cœur* /lə kœʁ/: one syllable, the open /œ/ of *sœur* /sœʁ/ and *peur* /pœʁ/ before a said *r*. The *œ* is one letter, the ligature, not *o* and *e*: *coeur* without it is a typing shortcut, seen on keyboards that lack the key.
- *Le* /lə/ says the noun is masculine: *le cœur, les cœurs* /lə kœʁ, le kœʁ/, *mon cœur* /mɔ̃ kœʁ/ my heart, and *mon cœur* is also *my darling*.
- *Par cœur* /paʁ kœʁ/ is *by heart*: *j'ai appris la leçon par cœur* /ʒe apʁi la ləsɔ̃ paʁ kœʁ/, the phrase of [par cœur](../../06_Expressions/par_coeur/README.md) in the Expressions chapter.
- *Le chœur* /lə kœʁ/ the choir is its homophone, with an *h* from the Greek; *en chœur* /ɑ̃ kœʁ/ in chorus.

## AI section: grammar, phrases, examples, exercises

- **The senses.** The organ, *le cœur bat* /lə kœʁ ba/ the heart beats; the feelings, *un cœur d'or* /œ̃ kœʁ dɔʁ/ a heart of gold; the centre, *au cœur de la ville* /o kœʁ də la vil/ in the heart of the town, *le cœur du problème* /lə kœʁ dy pʁɔblɛm/; courage, *perdre cœur* /pɛʁdʁ kœʁ/ to lose heart; the suit of cards, *la dame de cœur* /la dam də kœʁ/ the queen of hearts.
- **Avoir mal au cœur is not heart pain.** *J'ai mal au cœur* /ʒe mal o kœʁ/ is *I feel sick*, the stomach; the heart that hurts in love is *avoir le cœur brisé* /avwaʁ lə kœʁ bʁize/, and a heart attack is *une crise cardiaque* /yn kʁiz kaʁdjak/. The adjective of the heart is *cardiaque* /kaʁdjak/, from the Greek, as English *cardiac*.
- **Œ in other words.** /œ/ in *la sœur* /la sœʁ/, *un œuf* /œ̃n‿œf/, *un bœuf* /œ̃ bœf/, *l'œil* /lœj/; /ø/ in *des œufs* /dez‿ø/, *des bœufs* /de bø/, *le vœu* /lə vø/; /e/ in *l'œsophage* /lezɔfaʒ/.
