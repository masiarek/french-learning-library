The present of aller, all six forms	je vais, tu vas, il va, nous allons, vous allez, ils vont /ʒə vɛ, ty va, il va, nuz‿alɔ̃, vuz‿ale, il vɔ̃/	grammar
The three irregular forms the app flags, and what they are	vais /vɛ/ the je present, irai /iʁe/ the future, aille /aj/ the subjunctive: three Latin verbs in one	grammar
Which auxiliary does aller take, and what follows?	Être, and the participle agrees: je suis allé, elle est allée, ils sont allés /ʒə sɥi(z)‿ale, ɛl ɛ(t)‿ale, il sɔ̃(t)‿ale/, all alike to the ear	grammar
The near future: how is it built?	Aller in the present plus an infinitive: je vais manger /ʒə vɛ mɑ̃ʒe/ I am going to eat; its mirror is venir de, the recent past	grammar
Why vas-y with an s, when the imperative is va?	The s comes back before y for the liaison: vas-y /vazi/; alone it is va /va/	grammar
I am well: être or aller?	Aller: je vais bien /ʒə vɛ bjɛ̃/; être is for places and qualities, je suis à Paris, je suis prêt	grammar
