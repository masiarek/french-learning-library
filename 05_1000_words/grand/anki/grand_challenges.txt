The four forms of grand, with IPA	grand /ɡʁɑ̃/, grande /ɡʁɑ̃d/, grands /ɡʁɑ̃/, grandes /ɡʁɑ̃d/: the feminine -e makes the d sound, the plural -s is silent	grammar
A tall man and a great man: which word order?	un homme grand /œ̃n‿ɔm ɡʁɑ̃/ is tall; un grand homme /œ̃ ɡʁɑ̃t‿ɔm/ is great	grammar
What happens to the d of grand before a vowel?	It is said as a /t/: un grand homme /œ̃ ɡʁɑ̃t‿ɔm/, un grand arbre /œ̃ ɡʁɑ̃t‿aʁbʁ/; in the plural the s gives /z/: de grands arbres /də ɡʁɑ̃z‿aʁbʁ/	sound
Big houses: why de and not des?	Before a plural adjective that precedes its noun, careful French writes de: de grandes maisons /də ɡʁɑ̃d mɛzɔ̃/; after the noun des stays: des maisons confortables /de mɛzɔ̃ kɔ̃fɔʁtabl/	grammar
