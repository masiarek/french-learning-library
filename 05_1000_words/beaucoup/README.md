# beaucoup — a lot

## The word

Conventions: on the [chapter page](../README.md).

- *beaucoup* /boku/: *eau* is /o/, as in *eau* /o/ water and *bureau* /byʁo/, *ou* is /u/, and the final *p* is silent and stays silent before a vowel in everyday speech, *beaucoup aimé* /boku eme/. Two syllables, two rounded vowels, the closed /o/ and then /u/.
- An adverb, and it never changes: *j'aime beaucoup* /ʒɛm boku/ I like a lot, *il travaille beaucoup* /il tʁavaj boku/ he works a lot, *merci beaucoup* /mɛʁsi boku/ thank you very much. It goes after the verb, not before it as English *much* can.
- Before a noun it needs *de*, and the noun loses its article: *beaucoup de livres* /boku də livʁ/ many books, *beaucoup de temps* /boku də tɑ̃/ a lot of time, *beaucoup d'amis* /boku dami/ many friends. Never *beaucoup des livres* for *many books*; *beaucoup des* is only *many of the*, when a particular set is meant.
- *Beaucoup* is *much* and *many*; *very* is another word, *très* /tʁɛ/, which goes with an adjective or an adverb: *très grand* /tʁɛ ɡʁɑ̃/ very big, *très bien* /tʁɛ bjɛ̃/ very well. *J'aime beaucoup* /ʒɛm boku/ I like very much takes *beaucoup*, because *aimer* is a verb; *très* cannot follow a verb.
- The worksheet's model text ends with it, in the teacher's [La maison](../../02_Yosser_teacher/lesson_01_la_maison/README.md): *J'aime beaucoup ma maison.* /ʒɛm boku ma mɛzɔ̃/.

## AI section: grammar, phrases, examples, exercises

- **Beaucoup or très.** *Beaucoup* goes with a verb or a noun, *très* with an adjective or an adverb: *il mange beaucoup* /il mɑ̃ʒ boku/ he eats a lot, *il est très grand* /il ɛ tʁɛ ɡʁɑ̃/ he is very tall, *elle chante très bien* /ɛl ʃɑ̃t tʁɛ bjɛ̃/ she sings very well. The feelings that English puts with *very* and French with *avoir*, as the [avoir](../avoir/README.md) page says, take *très* all the same: *j'ai très faim* /ʒe tʁɛ fɛ̃/ I am very hungry, *j'ai très froid* /ʒe tʁɛ fʁwa/.
- **The words of quantity all take de.** *Beaucoup de* /boku də/ a lot of, *un peu de* /œ̃ pø də/ a little, *peu de* /pø də/ little, few, *assez de* /ase də/ enough, *trop de* /tʁo də/ too much, too many, *tant de* /tɑ̃ də/ so much, *plein de* /plɛ̃ də/ loads of, in speech, *combien de* /kɔ̃bjɛ̃ də/ how much, how many. The noun after them has no article: *trop de bruit* /tʁo də bʁɥi/ too much noise, *combien de chambres ?* /kɔ̃bjɛ̃ də ʃɑ̃bʁ/ how many bedrooms? The one exception is the particular set: *beaucoup des livres de cette liste* /boku de livʁ də sɛt list/ many of the books on this list.
- **Beaucoup alone, and with a pronoun.** With no noun the word stands alone: *il y a beaucoup à faire* /il j a boku a fɛʁ/ there is a lot to do, *beaucoup pensent que* /boku pɑ̃s kə/ many think that. The noun already named comes back as *en* /ɑ̃/: *des livres ? j'en ai beaucoup* /de livʁ, ʒɑ̃n‿e boku/ books? I have a lot of them. *Beaucoup de monde* /boku də mɔ̃d/ is a lot of people, *il y a beaucoup de monde* /il j a boku də mɔ̃d/ it is crowded.
- **Beaucoup with plus, trop, mieux.** *Beaucoup plus grand* /boku ply ɡʁɑ̃/ much bigger, *beaucoup moins cher* /boku mwɛ̃ ʃɛʁ/ much cheaper, *beaucoup mieux* /boku mjø/ much better, *beaucoup trop* /boku tʁo/ far too much, *beaucoup trop de bruit* /boku tʁo də bʁɥi/ far too much noise; *de beaucoup* /də boku/ by far: *il est de beaucoup le meilleur* /il ɛ də boku lə mɛjœʁ/ he is by far the best.
- **The scale of amount.** *Pas du tout* /pa dy tu/ not at all, *un peu* /œ̃ pø/ a little, *assez* /ase/ enough, fairly, *beaucoup* /boku/ a lot, *énormément* /enɔʁmemɑ̃/ enormously, *trop* /tʁo/ too much. *Pas beaucoup* /pa boku/ not much, not many: *je ne lis pas beaucoup* /ʒə nə li pa boku/ I don't read much.
