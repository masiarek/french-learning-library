Why do le lit and il lit sound the same?	The final t of lit is silent, so both are /li/; the article or the pronoun tells the bed from he reads	sound
lit against lu: which vowels?	/li/ bed against /ly/ read, the participle: the /i/ of si and the /y/ of tu	sound
To go to bed, to get out of bed, to make one's bed	aller au lit /ale o li/, sortir du lit /sɔʁtiʁ dy li/, faire son lit /fɛʁ sɔ̃ li/	words
Why au lit and du lit?	à + le = au /o/, de + le = du /dy/: the contracted article before a masculine noun	grammar
