# dans — in, inside

## The word

Conventions: on the [chapter page](../README.md).

- *dans* /dɑ̃/: a /d/ and the nasal vowel of *blanc* /blɑ̃/, drilled in [Les sons ɑ̃ et ɔ̃](../../04_Pronunciation/sons_an_on/README.md). The *-s* is silent before a consonant, *dans la cuisine* /dɑ̃ la kɥizin/, and said as /z/ before a vowel, *dans un endroit* /dɑ̃z‿œ̃n‿ɑ̃dʁwa/, *dans une maison* /dɑ̃z‿yn mɛzɔ̃/: after *dans* the liaison is compulsory.
- Three words sound the same: *dans* /dɑ̃/ in, *dent* /dɑ̃/ tooth and *d'en* /dɑ̃/ of it, from there; the sentence tells them apart, as the lesson [Homophones](../../01_Sounds/homophones/README.md) says. The neighbour to keep apart by ear is *dont* /dɔ̃/ whose, with the other nasal.
- *Dans* is inside: *dans la maison* /dɑ̃ la mɛzɔ̃/, *dans ma chambre* /dɑ̃ ma ʃɑ̃bʁ/, *dans le sac* /dɑ̃ lə sak/. For a town or a country French says *à* /a/ and *en* /ɑ̃/ instead, *à Paris* /a paʁi/, *en Pologne* /ɑ̃ pɔlɔɲ/, and for *at home* it says *à la maison* /a la mɛzɔ̃/; *dans la maison* /dɑ̃ la mɛzɔ̃/ means inside the walls. The AI section sorts the three out.
- In time, *dans* means from now: *dans dix minutes* /dɑ̃ di minyt/ in ten minutes' time. And where English takes something *from* or *out of* a container, French takes it *dans* the container: *boire dans un verre* /bwaʁ dɑ̃z‿œ̃ vɛʁ/ to drink out of a glass.
- The word is the first of the key words of section 9 of the teacher's [La maison](../../02_Yosser_teacher/lesson_01_la_maison/README.md), and opens every sentence of its exercise 8: *Dans ma maison, il y a un salon.* /dɑ̃ ma mɛzɔ̃, il j a œ̃ salɔ̃/.

## AI section: grammar, phrases, examples, exercises

- **Dans, sur, sous.** Inside, on, under: *dans la boîte* /dɑ̃ la bwat/ in the box, *sur la boîte* /syʁ la bwat/ on the box, *sous la boîte* /su la bwat/ under the box. [Sur](../sur/README.md) and [sous](../sous/README.md) have pages of their own; the five prepositions of place of the worksheet are *dans, sur, sous, devant, derrière* /dɑ̃, syʁ, su, dəvɑ̃, dɛʁjɛʁ/ and *à côté de* /a kote də/.
- **Dans, à or en for a place.** *Dans* is inside something with walls or edges. *À* is the point on the map or the place as an institution: *à la maison* /a la mɛzɔ̃/ at home, *à l'école* /a lekɔl/ at school, *au bureau* /o byʁo/ at the office, *au lit* /o li/ in bed, *à Paris* /a paʁi/. *En* is a feminine country or a state: *en France* /ɑ̃ fʁɑ̃s/, *en Pologne* /ɑ̃ pɔlɔɲ/, *en ville* /ɑ̃ vil/ in town, *en classe* /ɑ̃ klas/ in class; a masculine country takes *au*, *au Portugal* /o pɔʁtyɡal/. So *je suis à la maison* /ʒə sɥi a la mɛzɔ̃/ I am at home, but *le chat est dans la maison* /lə ʃa ɛ dɑ̃ la mɛzɔ̃/ the cat is inside the house. Some *dans* answer to an English *on*: *dans la rue* /dɑ̃ la ʁy/ in the street, *dans le train* /dɑ̃ lə tʁɛ̃/ on the train, *dans l'avion* /dɑ̃ lavjɔ̃/ on the plane, *dans le journal* /dɑ̃ lə ʒuʁnal/ in the paper.
- **Dans for time: from now.** *Dans dix minutes* /dɑ̃ di minyt/ is ten minutes from now, *dans une semaine* /dɑ̃z‿yn səmɛn/ a week from now. The time a thing takes is *en*: *en dix minutes* /ɑ̃ di minyt/ in ten minutes, that is, within ten minutes. Ten minutes ago is *il y a dix minutes* /il j a di minyt/, with the *il y a* of the [avoir](../avoir/README.md) page.
- **Dans where English says from.** The thing is in the container when you take it, so French names the container: *boire dans un verre* /bwaʁ dɑ̃z‿œ̃ vɛʁ/ to drink out of a glass, *prendre un livre dans le sac* /pʁɑ̃dʁ œ̃ livʁ dɑ̃ lə sak/ to take a book out of the bag, *lire dans le journal* /liʁ dɑ̃ lə ʒuʁnal/ to read in the paper, *manger dans une assiette* /mɑ̃ʒe dɑ̃z‿yn asjɛt/ to eat off a plate.
- **The adverb.** With no noun after it the word is *dedans* /dədɑ̃/ inside: *il est dedans* /il ɛ dədɑ̃/ he is inside, *là-dedans* /ladədɑ̃/ in there, *en dedans* /ɑ̃ dədɑ̃/ on the inside. The opposite pair is *dehors* /dəɔʁ/ outside and *en dehors de* /ɑ̃ dəɔʁ də/ outside of.
- **About, roughly.** *Dans les dix euros* /dɑ̃ le diz‿øʁo/ around ten euros, *dans la trentaine* /dɑ̃ la tʁɑ̃tɛn/ in one's thirties.
