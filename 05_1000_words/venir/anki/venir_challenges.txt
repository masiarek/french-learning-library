The present of venir, all six forms	je viens, tu viens, il vient, nous venons, vous venez, ils viennent /ʒə vjɛ̃, ty vjɛ̃, il vjɛ̃, nu vənɔ̃, vu vəne, il vjɛn/	grammar
vient or viennent: what happens to the nasal?	vient /vjɛ̃/ ends in the nasal vowel; viennent /vjɛn/ has an oral vowel and the n said, because a vowel follows in the spelling (denasalisation)	sound
The recent past: how is it built?	Venir in the present, de, and an infinitive: je viens de finir /ʒə vjɛ̃ də finiʁ/ I have just finished; the mirror of aller plus infinitive	grammar
Which auxiliary does venir take?	Être, with agreement: je suis venu, elle est venue /ʒə sɥi vəny, ɛl ɛ vəny/	grammar
The future of venir	je viendrai /ʒə vjɛ̃dʁe/, on the stem viendr-; the conditional je viendrais /ʒə vjɛ̃dʁɛ/	grammar
Four verbs of the family of venir, with the je form	tenir, je tiens /tjɛ̃/; devenir, je deviens /dəvjɛ̃/; revenir, je reviens /ʁəvjɛ̃/; se souvenir, je me souviens /suvjɛ̃/	words
