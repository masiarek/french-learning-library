# derrière — behind

## The word

Conventions: on the [chapter page](../README.md).

- *derrière* /dɛʁjɛʁ/: two open /ɛ/, each before a consonant that is said, as the [loi de position](../../04_Pronunciation/son_e_ouvert/README.md) wants; the double *rr* is one /ʁ/; and *-ière* is /jɛʁ/ as in *première* /pʁəmjɛʁ/, *bière* /bjɛʁ/ and *lumière* /lymjɛʁ/. The final *-e* is silent and the *r* before it is said, so the word runs straight on to the next, *derrière une porte* /dɛʁjɛʁ yn pɔʁt/, with no liaison to think about.
- *Derrière* is behind, in space: *derrière la maison* /dɛʁjɛʁ la mɛzɔ̃/, *derrière la porte* /dɛʁjɛʁ la pɔʁt/, *derrière moi* /dɛʁjɛʁ mwa/. Its opposite is [devant](../devant/README.md) /dəvɑ̃/. For *after* in time French says *après* /apʁɛ/: *après le dîner* /apʁɛ lə dine/ after dinner, *derrière la porte* /dɛʁjɛʁ la pɔʁt/ behind the door.
- Without a noun it is an adverb, *il est derrière* /il ɛ dɛʁjɛʁ/ he is behind, at the back; with an article a noun, *le derrière* /lə dɛʁjɛʁ/ the back, and in everyday talk the behind one sits on.
- The back of a car, a train or a house has its own word, *l'arrière* /laʁjɛʁ/: *le siège arrière* /lə sjɛʒ aʁjɛʁ/ the back seat, *à l'arrière* /a laʁjɛʁ/ at the back. *Derrière* says where a thing is in relation to another; *arrière* names the back part itself.
- It is the fifth key word of section 9 of the teacher's [La maison](../../02_Yosser_teacher/lesson_01_la_maison/README.md): *Le jardin est derrière la maison.* /lə ʒaʁdɛ̃ ɛ dɛʁjɛʁ la mɛzɔ̃/.

## AI section: grammar, phrases, examples, exercises

- **Derrière and devant.** *Le jardin est derrière la maison* /lə ʒaʁdɛ̃ ɛ dɛʁjɛʁ la mɛzɔ̃/, *la voiture est devant la maison* /la vwatyʁ ɛ dəvɑ̃ la mɛzɔ̃/. In a queue or a race *derrière* is behind in the order: *il est derrière moi* /il ɛ dɛʁjɛʁ mwa/ he is behind me, *l'un derrière l'autre* /lœ̃ dɛʁjɛʁ lotʁ/ one behind the other.
- **Derrière or après.** Space against time, like *devant* against *avant*. *Derrière la gare* /dɛʁjɛʁ la ɡaʁ/ behind the station; *après la gare* /apʁɛ la ɡaʁ/ after the station, further along the road; *après le dîner* /apʁɛ lə dine/ after dinner. So the four words go in two pairs: *devant, derrière* /dəvɑ̃, dɛʁjɛʁ/ for space, *avant, après* /avɑ̃, apʁɛ/ for time.
- **Derrière or arrière.** *Derrière* places a thing behind another; *arrière* /aʁjɛʁ/ is the back part of a thing: *le siège arrière* /lə sjɛʒ aʁjɛʁ/ the back seat, *la roue arrière* /la ʁu aʁjɛʁ/ the back wheel, *à l'arrière de la voiture* /a laʁjɛʁ də la vwatyʁ/ at the back of the car, *en arrière* /ɑ̃n‿aʁjɛʁ/ backwards, *faire marche arrière* /fɛʁ maʁʃ aʁjɛʁ/ to reverse. *La porte de derrière* /la pɔʁt də dɛʁjɛʁ/ the back door uses *derrière* as an adjective in the same way as *la porte de devant* /la pɔʁt də dəvɑ̃/.
- **Behind someone.** *Se cacher derrière* /sə kaʃe dɛʁjɛʁ/ to hide behind, *regarder derrière soi* /ʁəɡaʁde dɛʁjɛʁ swa/ to look behind oneself, *laisser derrière soi* /lese dɛʁjɛʁ swa/ to leave behind, *fermer la porte derrière soi* /fɛʁme la pɔʁt dɛʁjɛʁ swa/ to close the door behind oneself, *avoir toute l'équipe derrière soi* /avwaʁ tut lekip dɛʁjɛʁ swa/ to have the whole team behind one, that is, on one's side; *par-derrière* /paʁdɛʁjɛʁ/ from behind, and behind one's back.
- **What is behind it.** *Il y a quelque chose derrière* /il j a kɛlkə ʃoz dɛʁjɛʁ/ there is something behind this, *derrière les mots* /dɛʁjɛʁ le mo/ behind the words, *derrière les barreaux* /dɛʁjɛʁ le baʁo/ behind bars.
