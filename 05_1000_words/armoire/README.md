# une armoire — a wardrobe

## The word

Conventions: on the [chapter page](../README.md).

- *une armoire* /yn aʁmwaʁ/: *oi* is /wa/, as in *moi* /mwa/, *trois* /tʁwa/, *la voiture* /la vwatyʁ/, and the word ends in the French r. Both *r*'s of the spelling are said.
- *Une* /yn/ says the noun is feminine, and the vowel at its front changes the small words before it: *l'armoire* /laʁmwaʁ/ by elision, *les armoires* /lez‿aʁmwaʁ/ and *des armoires* /dez‿aʁmwaʁ/ by liaison, and *mon armoire* /mɔ̃n‿aʁmwaʁ/, where the feminine *ma* gives way to *mon* for the sound, as *mon amie* on the page of [une mère](../mere/README.md).
- *Une armoire* is a wardrobe, the tall cupboard for clothes; *un placard* /œ̃ plakaʁ/ is a cupboard built into the wall, *une commode* /yn kɔmɔd/ a chest of drawers, *une étagère* /yn etaʒɛʁ/ a shelf. The furniture of Adam's bedroom, *un lit, un bureau, une chaise et une armoire* /œ̃ li, œ̃ byʁo, yn ʃɛz e yn aʁmwaʁ/, is in [La maison](../../02_Yosser_teacher/lesson_01_la_maison/README.md), and each piece has a page now.
- The verb of the wardrobe is *ranger* /ʁɑ̃ʒe/, to put away, to tidy: *ranger ses vêtements dans l'armoire* /ʁɑ̃ʒe se vɛtmɑ̃ dɑ̃ laʁmwaʁ/.

## AI section: grammar, phrases, examples, exercises

- **Ranger.** A regular *-er* verb with one spelling trick: *nous rangeons* /nu ʁɑ̃ʒɔ̃/ keeps the *e* so that the *g* stays soft, like *nous mangeons* /nu mɑ̃ʒɔ̃/. *Je range* /ʒə ʁɑ̃ʒ/, *tu ranges* /ty ʁɑ̃ʒ/, *il range* /il ʁɑ̃ʒ/, *vous rangez* /vu ʁɑ̃ʒe/, *ils rangent* /il ʁɑ̃ʒ/; past participle *rangé* /ʁɑ̃ʒe/; *Range ta chambre !* /ʁɑ̃ʒ ta ʃɑ̃bʁ/ Tidy your room! The other verbs of the wardrobe: *accrocher* /akʁɔʃe/ to hang up, *plier* /plije/ to fold, *sortir* /sɔʁtiʁ/ to take out.
- **In the wardrobe.** *Les vêtements* /le vɛtmɑ̃/ the clothes, *un cintre* /œ̃ sɛ̃tʁ/ a coat hanger, *une étagère* /yn etaʒɛʁ/ a shelf, *un tiroir* /œ̃ tiʁwaʁ/ a drawer, *une robe* /yn ʁɔb/ a dress, *un pantalon* /œ̃ pɑ̃talɔ̃/ trousers, *une chemise* /yn ʃəmiz/ a shirt, *un pull* /œ̃ pyl/ a jumper, *un manteau* /œ̃ mɑ̃to/ a coat, *des chaussures* /de ʃosyʁ/ shoes.
- **The cupboards.** *Un placard* /œ̃ plakaʁ/ a built-in cupboard, *un placard de cuisine* /œ̃ plakaʁ də kɥizin/ a kitchen cupboard, *une commode* /yn kɔmɔd/ a chest of drawers, *un buffet* /œ̃ byfɛ/ a sideboard, *une bibliothèque* /yn biblijɔtɛk/ a bookcase, *une armoire à pharmacie* /yn aʁmwaʁ a faʁmasi/ a medicine cabinet.
- **Armoire à glace.** *Une armoire à glace* /yn aʁmwaʁ a ɡlas/ is a wardrobe with a mirror in its door and, of a man, a hulk, built like a wardrobe. *Une glace* /yn ɡlas/ is a mirror, an ice cream and ice.
- **Before the vowel.** *L'armoire, les armoires, mon armoire, cette armoire* /sɛt aʁmwaʁ/, *une grande armoire* /yn ɡʁɑ̃d aʁmwaʁ/, *une vieille armoire* /yn vjɛj aʁmwaʁ/: the feminine adjective already ends in a consonant that is said, so the vowel changes nothing there. The one word that changes its form is *ma*, which becomes *mon*.
