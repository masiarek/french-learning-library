oi in armoire: which sound?	/wa/, as in moi, trois, voiture: /aʁmwaʁ/, with both r's said	sound
Why mon armoire for a feminine noun?	Before a vowel the feminine ma becomes mon for the sound: mon armoire /mɔ̃n‿aʁmwaʁ/, as mon amie	grammar
The wardrobe, the wardrobes, with the article	l'armoire /laʁmwaʁ/ by elision, les armoires /lez‿aʁmwaʁ/ by liaison	grammar
We tidy: why the e in rangeons?	nous rangeons /nu ʁɑ̃ʒɔ̃/ keeps the e so that the g stays soft, like nous mangeons	grammar
