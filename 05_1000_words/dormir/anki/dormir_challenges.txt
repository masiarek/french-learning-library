The present of dormir, all six forms	je dors, tu dors, il dort, nous dormons, vous dormez, ils dorment /ʒə dɔʁ, ty dɔʁ, il dɔʁ, nu dɔʁmɔ̃, vu dɔʁme, il dɔʁm/	grammar
Why je dors and not je dormis?	Dormir follows partir, not finir: the singular of the present drops the stem’s last consonant (dors, dort /dɔʁ/) and the plural keeps it (dormons /dɔʁmɔ̃/); finir inserts -ss- instead (nous finissons)	grammar
Five verbs that conjugate like dormir, with the je form	partir, je pars /paʁ/; sortir, je sors /sɔʁ/; sentir, je sens /sɑ̃/; servir, je sers /sɛʁ/; mentir, je mens /mɑ̃/	grammar
Where does bien go in a compound tense?	Between the auxiliary and the participle: j’ai bien dormi /ʒe bjɛ̃ dɔʁmi/, as-tu bien dormi ?	grammar
