Say: Who is at the door?	Qui est à la porte ? /ki ɛ a la pɔʁt/	say
Why /pɔʁt/ with an open o?	A consonant that is said follows the o, so it stays open, as in bol /bɔl/; pot /po/ closes because nothing follows	sound
la porte and il porte	the door /la pɔʁt/ and he carries, he wears /il pɔʁt/, from porter: the same sound, told apart by the article or the pronoun	grammar
