# une porte — a door

## The word

Conventions: on the [chapter page](../README.md).

- *une porte* /yn pɔʁt/: the *o* is the open /ɔ/ because a consonant that is said follows it, as in *bol* /bɔl/ and *sort* /sɔʁ/; with nothing after it, *o* closes to /o/, *pot* /po/, *mot* /mo/. The final *-e* is silent and makes the *t* sound, as the *-e* of *chaise* makes its *s* sound.
- *Une* /yn/ says the noun is feminine: *la porte* /la pɔʁt/, *les portes* /le pɔʁt/.
- *Porte* is also a verb form: *il porte* /il pɔʁt/ he carries, he wears, from *porter* /pɔʁte/. *Elle porte un manteau* /ɛl pɔʁt œ̃ mɑ̃to/ she is wearing a coat. The article or the pronoun says which *porte* it is.
- At the door one knocks, rings, opens and locks: *frapper à la porte* /fʁape a la pɔʁt/, *sonner à la porte* /sɔne a la pɔʁt/, *ouvrir la porte* /uvʁiʁ la pɔʁt/, *fermer la porte à clé* /fɛʁme la pɔʁt a kle/. *Ouvrir* and *fermer* are conjugated on the page of [une fenêtre](../fenetre/README.md).

## AI section: grammar, phrases, examples, exercises

- **À la porte.** *À la porte* /a la pɔʁt/ is at the door, and in *mettre quelqu'un à la porte* /mɛtʁ kɛlkœ̃ a la pɔʁt/ it is to throw someone out, of the house or of a job. *Qui est à la porte ?* /ki ɛ a la pɔʁt/ Who is at the door? *On frappe à la porte* /ɔ̃ fʁap a la pɔʁt/ someone is knocking.
- **The doors of a house.** *La porte d'entrée* /la pɔʁt dɑ̃tʁe/ the front door, *la porte de derrière* /la pɔʁt də dɛʁjɛʁ/ the back door, *la porte du garage* /la pɔʁt dy ɡaʁaʒ/, *la porte de la chambre* /la pɔʁt də la ʃɑ̃bʁ/, *le portail* /lə pɔʁtaj/ the gate. On the door: *la poignée* /la pwaɲe/ the handle, *la serrure* /la seʁyʁ/ the lock, *la clé* /la kle/ the key, *la sonnette* /la sɔnɛt/ the bell, and under it *le paillasson* /lə pajasɔ̃/, the doormat.
- **Open or closed.** *La porte est ouverte* /la pɔʁt ɛt‿uvɛʁt/, *fermée* /fɛʁme/, *fermée à clé* /fɛʁme a kle/ locked, *entrouverte* /ɑ̃tʁuvɛʁt/ ajar; *laisser la porte ouverte* /lese la pɔʁt uvɛʁt/ to leave the door open; *claquer la porte* /klake la pɔʁt/ to slam the door.
- **Porte in the city and at the airport.** The old city gates gave Paris its *portes*, *la porte de Versailles* /la pɔʁt də vɛʁsaj/, *la porte d'Orléans* /la pɔʁt dɔʁleɑ̃/, metro stations now. At the airport the gate is *la porte d'embarquement* /la pɔʁt dɑ̃baʁkəmɑ̃/, *porte douze* /pɔʁt duz/.
- **Set phrases.** *C'est la porte à côté* /sɛ la pɔʁt a kote/ it is right next door; *entre deux portes* /ɑ̃tʁ dø pɔʁt/ in passing, in a hurry; *trouver porte close* /tʁuve pɔʁt kloz/ to find nobody in; *la journée portes ouvertes* /la ʒuʁne pɔʁt uvɛʁt/ the open day; *faire du porte-à-porte* /fɛʁ dy pɔʁtapɔʁt/ to go door to door.
