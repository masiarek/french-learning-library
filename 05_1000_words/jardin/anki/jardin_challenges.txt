Say: There is no garden, but there is a balcony.	Il n’y a pas de jardin, mais il y a un balcon. /il nj a pa də ʒaʁdɛ̃, mɛ il j a œ̃ balkɔ̃/	say
Why is jardin /ʒaʁdɛ̃/ but jardiner /ʒaʁdine/?	At the end of a word -in is the nasal vowel /ɛ̃/ and no n is said; before a vowel the n is said and the vowel turns oral, as in cousin, cousine /kuzɛ̃, kuzin/	sound
