Say: Please sit down. (politely)	Asseyez-vous, je vous en prie. /asejevu, ʒə vuz‿ɑ̃ pʁi/	say

Say: There are not enough chairs for everyone.	Il n’y a pas assez de chaises pour tout le monde. /il nj a pa ase də ʃɛz puʁ tu lə mɔ̃d/	say
Why is chaise /ʃɛz/ but chez /ʃe/?	The e is open before a consonant that is said and closed with nothing after it; the final -e of chaise is silent but makes the s sound, as /z/	sound
