# un tapis — a carpet, a rug

## The word

Conventions: on the [chapter page](../README.md).

- *un tapis* /œ̃ tapi/: the final *s* is silent, so the word ends in /i/ like *lit*. Written with its *s*, it does not change in the plural, *des tapis* /de tapi/, like every noun in *-s, -x* or *-z*: *un fils, des fils* /fis/, *un prix, des prix* /pʁi/, *un nez, des nez* /ne/.
- *Un* /œ̃/ says the noun is masculine: *le tapis* /lə tapi/, *les tapis* /le tapi/.
- *Un tapis* is a loose carpet or a rug. The fitted carpet is *la moquette* /la mɔkɛt/, the mat at the door *le paillasson* /lə pajasɔ̃/. What a rug lies on: *le sol* /lə sɔl/ the floor, *le parquet* /lə paʁkɛ/ the wooden floor, *le carrelage* /lə kaʁlaʒ/ the tiled floor.
- *Tapis* keeps company with [sur](../sur/README.md): *sur le tapis* /syʁ lə tapi/ on the rug, and in the idiom *mettre une question sur le tapis* /mɛtʁ yn kɛstjɔ̃ syʁ lə tapi/, to bring a question up, the *tapis* is the cloth of the meeting table, the word's first meaning.

## AI section: grammar, phrases, examples, exercises

- **The plural that does not change.** A noun that already ends in *-s, -x* or *-z* takes no plural mark: *le bras, les bras* /bʁa/, *la voix, les voix* /vwa/, *le gaz, les gaz* /ɡaz/. Only the article says the number, and before a vowel the liaison: *les tapis* /le tapi/, *les enfants* /lez‿ɑ̃fɑ̃/. The nouns in *-eau* go the other way and add *-x*, *un bureau, des bureaux*, on the page of [un bureau](../bureau/README.md).
- **The floor and what covers it.** *Le sol* /lə sɔl/ is the floor and the ground; *le plancher* /lə plɑ̃ʃe/ and *le parquet* /lə paʁkɛ/ the wooden floor; *le carrelage* /lə kaʁlaʒ/ the tiles; *la moquette* /la mɔkɛt/ the fitted carpet; *un tapis* a rug on any of them; *le paillasson* the doormat, the rug one wipes one's feet on, *essuyer ses pieds* /esɥije se pje/.
- **Tapis away from the floor.** *Un tapis de bain* /œ̃ tapi də bɛ̃/ a bath mat, *un tapis de souris* /œ̃ tapi də suʁi/ a mouse mat, *un tapis de yoga* /œ̃ tapi də jɔɡa/, *un tapis roulant* /œ̃ tapi ʁulɑ̃/ a treadmill, a conveyor belt or a moving walkway, *un tapis volant* /œ̃ tapi vɔlɑ̃/ a flying carpet, *le tapis rouge* /lə tapi ʁuʒ/ the red carpet, *un tapis persan* /œ̃ tapi pɛʁsɑ̃/ a Persian rug.
- **Three words in /tapi/.** *Le tapis* /lə tapi/, *le tapir* /lə tapiʁ/ the animal, with its *r* said, and *il tape* /il tap/ he types or he hits, from *taper* /tape/. The silent *s* is what keeps *tapis* apart from *tapir* in the mouth: nothing after the /i/.
- **Cleaning it.** *Passer l'aspirateur* /pase laspiʁatœʁ/ to vacuum, *secouer le tapis* /səkwe lə tapi/ to shake the rug, *nettoyer* /nɛtwaje/ to clean, *une tache sur le tapis* /yn taʃ syʁ lə tapi/ a stain on the rug.
