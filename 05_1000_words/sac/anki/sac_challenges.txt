Is the c of sac said? And of blanc?	Yes, /sak/: a final c is said after an oral vowel (lac, parc, avec); silent after a nasal vowel, blanc /blɑ̃/, banc /bɑ̃/; tabac /taba/ and estomac /ɛstɔma/ are the exceptions	sound
