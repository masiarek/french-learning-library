Why do la pièce and les pièces sound the same?	Both are /pjɛs/: the plural s is silent, so only the article, la /la/ against les /le/, says how many	sound
Pièce, chambre, salle, place: which is which?	la pièce /la pjɛs/ any room; la chambre /la ʃɑ̃bʁ/ the bedroom; la salle /la sal/ a big room with a job (de bains, à manger, de classe); la place /la plas/ room as space, never a room of the house	words
What is un trois-pièces, and which rooms does it not count?	A flat with three main rooms, a living room and two bedrooms (T3, F3); the kitchen, the bathroom, the toilet, the entrance and the corridor are pièces de service and dégagements and are not counted	grammar
