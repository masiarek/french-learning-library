Sa mère: his mother or her mother?	Either: the possessive agrees with the noun owned (mère, feminine), not with the owner; Pierre et sa mère, Marie et sa mère /sa mɛʁ/	grammar
The possessives for my, your (tu), his or her, before père, mère, parents	mon, ma, mes /mɔ̃, ma, me/; ton, ta, tes /tɔ̃, ta, te/; son, sa, ses /sɔ̃, sa, se/	grammar
Why mon amie and not ma amie?	Before a vowel the feminine ma, ta, sa become mon, ton, son for the sound: mon amie /mɔ̃n‿ami/, son école /sɔ̃n‿ekɔl/	grammar
Marie’s mother, in French	la mère de Marie /la mɛʁ də maʁi/: possession with de, French has no ’s	grammar
français → française: what changes in sound?	The final consonant is said: /fʁɑ̃sɛ/ → /fʁɑ̃sɛz/; the same for polonais, polonaise /pɔlɔnɛ, pɔlɔnɛz/	sound
