The present of devoir, all six forms	je dois, tu dois, il doit, nous devons, vous devez, ils doivent /ʒə dwa, ty dwa, il dwa, nu dəvɔ̃, vu dəve, il dwav/	grammar
Why dû with a circumflex?	To tell it from du, the contracted article (le lit du garçon): dû /dy/ had to, du /dy/ of the; the feminine due and plurals dus, dues drop the accent	grammar
How do you say should in French?	With the conditional of devoir: je devrais /ʒə dəvʁɛ/ I should; and should have is j’aurais dû /ʒoʁɛ dy/	grammar
Il doit être malade: which must is this?	Probability, he must be ill; devoir covers both must of obligation (tu dois partir) and must of probability	grammar
Two senses of devoir in one sentence each	Je dois partir /ʒə dwa paʁtiʁ/ I have to leave; je te dois dix euros /ʒə tə dwa diz‿øʁo/ I owe you ten euros	words
The noun that looks like the verb	le devoir /lə dəvwaʁ/ duty, les devoirs /le dəvwaʁ/ homework: faire ses devoirs	words
