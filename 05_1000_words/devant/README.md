# devant — in front of

## The word

Conventions: on the [chapter page](../README.md).

- *devant* /dəvɑ̃/: the schwa of *de* /də/, then /v/ and the nasal vowel of *blanc* /blɑ̃/; the final *-t* is silent, *devant la maison* /dəvɑ̃ la mɛzɔ̃/, and the liaison before a vowel is optional, *devant une porte* /dəvɑ̃(t)‿yn pɔʁt/.
- *Devant* is in front of, in space: *devant la maison* /dəvɑ̃ la mɛzɔ̃/, *devant la porte* /dəvɑ̃ la pɔʁt/, *devant moi* /dəvɑ̃ mwa/. Its opposite is [derrière](../derriere/README.md) /dɛʁjɛʁ/ behind. The word for *before* in time is a different one, *avant* /avɑ̃/: *avant le dîner* /avɑ̃ lə dine/ before dinner, *devant la porte* /dəvɑ̃ la pɔʁt/ in front of the door. English *before* covers both; French keeps them apart.
- Without a noun it is an adverb, *il est devant* /il ɛ dəvɑ̃/ he is in front, and with an article a noun, *le devant de la maison* /lə dəvɑ̃ də la mɛzɔ̃/ the front of the house.
- The same four syllables are the present participle of [devoir](../devoir/README.md): *devant partir* /dəvɑ̃ paʁtiʁ/ having to leave. One spelling, two words.
- It is the fourth key word of section 9 of the teacher's [La maison](../../02_Yosser_teacher/lesson_01_la_maison/README.md): *La voiture est devant la maison.* /la vwatyʁ ɛ dəvɑ̃ la mɛzɔ̃/.

## AI section: grammar, phrases, examples, exercises

- **Devant and derrière.** The pair of the worksheet: *la voiture est devant la maison* /la vwatyʁ ɛ dəvɑ̃ la mɛzɔ̃/, *le jardin est derrière la maison* /lə ʒaʁdɛ̃ ɛ dɛʁjɛʁ la mɛzɔ̃/. In a queue or a race, *devant* is ahead: *il est devant moi* /il ɛ dəvɑ̃ mwa/ he is ahead of me, *passer devant tout le monde* /pase dəvɑ̃ tu lə mɔ̃d/ to jump the queue.
- **Devant or avant.** Space against time. *Devant la gare* /dəvɑ̃ la ɡaʁ/ in front of the station, *avant la gare* /avɑ̃ la ɡaʁ/ before the station, on the way; *devant le dîner* is not French, *avant le dîner* /avɑ̃ lə dine/ before dinner is. The pair of opposites is *devant, derrière* /dəvɑ̃, dɛʁjɛʁ/ for space and *avant, après* /avɑ̃, apʁɛ/ for time.
- **Devant or en face de.** *Devant* is in front, on the same side; *en face de* /ɑ̃ fas də/ is opposite, across from: *la boulangerie est en face de la gare* /la bulɑ̃ʒʁi ɛ(t)‿ɑ̃ fas də la ɡaʁ/ the bakery is opposite the station, *il attend devant la gare* /il atɑ̃ dəvɑ̃ la ɡaʁ/ he is waiting in front of the station.
- **In the presence of.** *Devant témoins* /dəvɑ̃ temwɛ̃/ before witnesses, *devant le juge* /dəvɑ̃ lə ʒyʒ/ before the judge, *devant la loi* /dəvɑ̃ la lwa/ before the law, *devant tout le monde* /dəvɑ̃ tu lə mɔ̃d/ in front of everybody, *devant le danger* /dəvɑ̃ lə dɑ̃ʒe/ in the face of danger.
- **The adverb and the noun.** *Devant* /dəvɑ̃/ in front, *par devant* /paʁ dəvɑ̃/ from the front, *droit devant* /dʁwa dəvɑ̃/ straight ahead, *le devant* /lə dəvɑ̃/ the front, *de devant* /də dəvɑ̃/ front as an adjective, *la porte de devant* /la pɔʁt də dəvɑ̃/ the front door, *les pattes de devant* /le pat də dəvɑ̃/ the front paws; *prendre les devants* /pʁɑ̃dʁ le dəvɑ̃/ to take the initiative, *aller au-devant de* /ale odəvɑ̃ də/ to go to meet.
