# la cuisine — the kitchen

## The word

Conventions: on the [chapter page](../README.md).

- *la cuisine* /la kɥizin/: *cui-* is /kɥi/, the glide /ɥ/ of *huit* /ɥit/ and *lui* /lɥi/ running into /i/, the way *oi* is /wa/, so *cuisine* starts like *le cuir* /lə kɥiʁ/ leather and *cuire* /kɥiʁ/ to cook, not like *la cousine* /la kuzin/ the cousin, which has the plain /u/. The *s* between two vowels is /z/, and the final *-e* is silent, so the word ends on /n/.
- *La* /la/ says the noun is feminine: *une cuisine* /yn kɥizin/, *une grande cuisine* /yn ɡʁɑ̃d kɥizin/, *une petite cuisine* /yn pətit kɥizin/, *les cuisines* /le kɥizin/; the plural *-s* is silent.
- Two things under one word, the room and what is done in it: *la cuisine* is the kitchen, *dans la cuisine, il y a une table* /dɑ̃ la kɥizin, il j a yn tabl/, and it is cooking, *faire la cuisine* /fɛʁ la kɥizin/ to cook, *la cuisine française* /la kɥizin fʁɑ̃sɛz/ French cooking, the cuisine English took as it is. The verb is *cuisiner* /kɥizine/, the person *le cuisinier, la cuisinière* /lə kɥizinje, la kɥizinjɛʁ/ the cook, and *la cuisinière* is the cooker too.
- The word comes from the teacher's first lesson, [La maison](../../02_Yosser_teacher/lesson_01_la_maison/README.md), among the rooms, *un salon, une cuisine, trois chambres* /œ̃ salɔ̃, yn kɥizin, tʁwa ʃɑ̃bʁ/, and in section 7, *Il y a une table dans la cuisine* /il j a yn tabl dɑ̃ la kɥizin/; its exercise 5 defines it, *la pièce où on prépare les repas* /la pjɛs u ɔ̃ pʁepaʁ le ʁəpa/, the room where the meals are prepared. Szypowska's [lesson 4](../../03_Szypowska/lecon_04_chez_les_lefevre/README.md) has it among the rooms of a flat, with *c* as /k/ before *u*.
- *Dans la cuisine* /dɑ̃ la kɥizin/ is in the kitchen, inside the room; *à la cuisine* /a la kɥizin/ is to the kitchen, or at the kitchen as the place where one works, *Va à la cuisine chercher du pain* /va a la kɥizin ʃɛʁʃe dy pɛ̃/; *en cuisine* /ɑ̃ kɥizin/, without an article, is in the kitchen of a restaurant, *le chef est en cuisine* /lə ʃɛf ɛt‿ɑ̃ kɥizin/.

## AI section: grammar, phrases, examples, exercises

- **Cuisiner and faire la cuisine.** Both mean to cook. *Faire la cuisine* /fɛʁ la kɥizin/ is the everyday phrase, *Qui fait la cuisine chez vous ?* /ki fɛ la kɥizin ʃe vu/ who does the cooking at your place?; *cuisiner* /kɥizine/ is a regular *-er* verb, *je cuisine* /ʒə kɥizin/, *nous cuisinons* /nu kɥizinɔ̃/, *ils cuisinent* /il kɥizin/, past participle *cuisiné* /kɥizine/, and it takes an object, *cuisiner un poulet* /kɥizine œ̃ pulɛ/ to cook a chicken. *Cuire* /kɥiʁ/ is what the food does, to cook in the heat, *le poulet cuit* /lə pulɛ kɥi/ the chicken is cooking; *faire cuire* /fɛʁ kɥiʁ/ is to cook it. *Il cuisine bien* /il kɥizin bjɛ̃/ he is a good cook.
- **The cook and the cooker.** *Le cuisinier* /lə kɥizinje/ is the cook, *la cuisinière* /la kɥizinjɛʁ/ the woman cook and the cooker, the stove: *une cuisinière à gaz* /yn kɥizinjɛʁ a ɡɑz/ a gas cooker, *une cuisinière électrique* /yn kɥizinjɛʁ elɛktʁik/. The sentence settles which. *Le chef* /lə ʃɛf/ is the head cook of a restaurant, *un chef cuisinier* /œ̃ ʃɛf kɥizinje/, and the word means the boss of anything, *le chef de bureau* /lə ʃɛf də byʁo/ on the page of [un bureau](../bureau/README.md).
- **The cuisine of a country.** *La cuisine française* /la kɥizin fʁɑ̃sɛz/, *la cuisine italienne* /la kɥizin italjɛn/, *la cuisine polonaise* /la kɥizin pɔlɔnɛz/, *la cuisine chinoise* /la kɥizin ʃinwaz/: the adjective is feminine, as *cuisine* is, with the endings of [une mère](../mere/README.md). *La bonne cuisine* /la bɔn kɥizin/ good cooking, *la cuisine maison* /la kɥizin mɛzɔ̃/ home cooking, *la nouvelle cuisine* /la nuvɛl kɥizin/, the light cooking of the 1970s, *un livre de cuisine* /œ̃ livʁ də kɥizin/ a cookbook, *une recette de cuisine* /yn ʁəsɛt də kɥizin/ a recipe, *un cours de cuisine* /œ̃ kuʁ də kɥizin/ a cooking class.
- **In the kitchen.** *Le four* /lə fuʁ/ the oven, *le frigo* /lə fʁiɡo/ the fridge, in full *le réfrigérateur* /lə ʁefʁiʒeʁatœʁ/, *l'évier* /levje/ the sink, which is *le lavabo* /lə lavabo/ in the bathroom, *le lave-vaisselle* /lə lavvɛsɛl/ the dishwasher, *le placard* /lə plakaʁ/ the cupboard, *le plan de travail* /lə plɑ̃ də tʁavaj/ the worktop, *une casserole* /yn kasʁɔl/ a saucepan, *une poêle* /yn pwal/ a frying pan, and *un poêle* /œ̃ pwal/, masculine, is a stove for heating. *Une cuisine équipée* /yn kɥizin ekipe/ is a fitted kitchen, *une cuisine américaine* /yn kɥizin ameʁikɛn/ an open-plan one, open onto the living room.
- **The meals.** *Un repas* /œ̃ ʁəpa/ a meal, *le petit déjeuner* /lə pəti deʒœne/ breakfast, *le déjeuner* /lə deʒœne/ lunch, *le dîner* /lə dine/ dinner, *préparer le repas* /pʁepaʁe lə ʁəpa/ to make the meal, *faire la vaisselle* /fɛʁ la vɛsɛl/ to wash up, *Bon appétit !* /bɔn‿apeti/ enjoy your meal, where *bon* loses its nasal before the vowel, as in *bon anniversaire* /bɔn‿anivɛʁsɛʁ/.
