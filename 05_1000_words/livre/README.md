# un livre — a book

## The word

Conventions: on the [chapter page](../README.md).

- *un livre* /œ̃ livʁ/: one syllable. The *i* is the /i/ of *un lit* /œ̃ li/, and the word ends on /vʁ/, the *v* and the French *r* said together with the *-e* silent, as in *vivre* /vivʁ/ to live and *le cuivre* /lə kɥivʁ/ copper. English *liver* has two syllables; French *livre* has one, and its *r* is the one at the back of the throat.
- *Un* /œ̃/ says the noun is masculine: *le livre* /lə livʁ/, *les livres* /le livʁ/, *des livres* /de livʁ/, *mes livres* /me livʁ/, *un bon livre* /œ̃ bɔ̃ livʁ/, *un gros livre* /œ̃ ɡʁo livʁ/. The plural *-s* is silent, so *des livres sur le bureau* /de livʁ syʁ lə byʁo/ is known to be plural from *des* alone.
- *La livre* /la livʁ/, feminine, is another word: the pound, both the weight, *une livre de beurre* /yn livʁ də bœʁ/ half a kilo of butter, and the money, *la livre anglaise* /la livʁ ɑ̃ɡlɛz/, *cent livres* /sɑ̃ livʁ/ a hundred pounds. The article is all that tells *le livre* from *la livre*, as it tells *le lit* /lə li/ from *la lie* /la li/ on the page of [la lie](../lie/README.md); and *il livre* /il livʁ/ he delivers, from *livrer* /livʁe/, sounds the same again.
- *Lire* /liʁ/ is the verb: *lire un livre* /liʁ œ̃ livʁ/ to read a book, *je lis* /ʒə li/, *il lit* /il li/, *j'ai lu* /ʒe ly/, conjugated on the page of [un lit](../lit/README.md). Two false friends wait by the book: *une librairie* /yn libʁɛʁi/ is a bookshop, where books are bought, and *une bibliothèque* /yn biblijɔtɛk/ is a library, where they are borrowed, and the bookcase at home.
- The word comes from the teacher's first lesson, [La maison](../../02_Yosser_teacher/lesson_01_la_maison/README.md): *Sur mon bureau, il y a mes livres et mes cahiers* /syʁ mɔ̃ byʁo, il j a me livʁ e me kaje/; in section 7, *Il y a des livres sur le bureau* /il j a de livʁ syʁ lə byʁo/; in section 9, *Le livre est sur le bureau* /lə livʁ ɛ syʁ lə byʁo/. The desk has its page, [un bureau](../bureau/README.md).

## AI section: grammar, phrases, examples, exercises

- **Le livre, la livre, il livre.** Three words, one sound, /livʁ/. *Le livre* the book, from Latin *liber*, the bark that was written on; *la livre* the pound, from *libra*, the scales and their weight, which also gave the sign £ and the Italian *lira*; *livrer* /livʁe/ to deliver, *je livre, il livre, ils livrent* /ʒə livʁ, il livʁ, il livʁ/, *nous livrons* /nu livʁɔ̃/, *livré* /livʁe/, with *la livraison* /la livʁɛzɔ̃/ the delivery and *le livreur* /lə livʁœʁ/ the delivery man. *Une livre* at the market is half a kilo, not the English 454 grams, and *une demi-livre* /yn dəmi livʁ/ a quarter.
- **Lire au présent.** *Je lis, tu lis, il lit* /ʒə li, ty li, il li/, *nous lisons, vous lisez, ils lisent* /nu lizɔ̃, vu lize, il liz/, past participle *lu* /ly/; *la lecture* /la lɛktyʁ/ is reading, *le lecteur, la lectrice* /lə lɛktœʁ, la lɛktʁis/ the reader. *Lire un livre* /liʁ œ̃ livʁ/, *ouvrir, fermer un livre* /uvʁiʁ, fɛʁme œ̃ livʁ/, *feuilleter un livre* /fœjte œ̃ livʁ/ to leaf through it, *emprunter un livre* /ɑ̃pʁœ̃te œ̃ livʁ/ to borrow it, *prêter un livre* /pʁɛte œ̃ livʁ/ to lend it, *rendre un livre* /ʁɑ̃dʁ œ̃ livʁ/ to give it back, *écrire un livre* /ekʁiʁ œ̃ livʁ/ to write one, *publier un livre* /pyblije œ̃ livʁ/ to publish it.
- **Librairie and bibliothèque.** *La librairie* /la libʁɛʁi/ is the bookshop and *le libraire* /lə libʁɛʁ/ the bookseller; *la bibliothèque* /la biblijɔtɛk/ is the library, *la bibliothèque municipale* /la biblijɔtɛk mynisipal/ the public library, with *le bibliothécaire* /lə biblijɔtekɛʁ/ the librarian; at home *la bibliothèque* is the bookcase, among the cupboards of [une armoire](../armoire/README.md). *Acheter un livre à la librairie* /aʃte œ̃ livʁ a la libʁɛʁi/, *emprunter un livre à la bibliothèque* /ɑ̃pʁœ̃te œ̃ livʁ a la biblijɔtɛk/. In speech a book is *un bouquin* /œ̃ bukɛ̃/, to read is *bouquiner* /bukine/, and *les bouquinistes* /le bukinist/ sell old books from green boxes along the Seine.
- **Kinds of book.** *Un livre de poche* /œ̃ livʁ də pɔʃ/ a paperback, a pocket book; *un livre de cuisine* /œ̃ livʁ də kɥizin/ a cookbook; *un livre d'images* /œ̃ livʁ dimaʒ/ a picture book; *un livre pour enfants* /œ̃ livʁ puʁ ɑ̃fɑ̃/ a children's book; *un livre d'or* /œ̃ livʁ dɔʁ/ a visitors' book; *un livre numérique* /œ̃ livʁ nymeʁik/ an e-book, *un livre audio* /œ̃ livʁ odjo/; *un manuel* /œ̃ manɥɛl/ a textbook, *un roman* /œ̃ ʁɔmɑ̃/ a novel, *un dictionnaire* /œ̃ diksjɔnɛʁ/, *une bande dessinée* /yn bɑ̃d desine/ a comic book, *une BD* /yn bede/ for short; *un cahier* /œ̃ kaje/ is a notebook, which is not a book.
- **The parts of a book.** *La couverture* /la kuvɛʁtyʁ/ the cover, *le titre* /lə titʁ/ the title, *l'auteur* /lotœʁ/ the author, *un écrivain* /œ̃n‿ekʁivɛ̃/ a writer, *une page* /yn paʒ/, *un chapitre* /œ̃ ʃapitʁ/, *la table des matières* /la tabl de matjɛʁ/ the contents, *le début* /lə deby/ the beginning, *la fin* /la fɛ̃/ the end.
