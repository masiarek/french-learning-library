The four forms of petit, with IPA	petit /pəti/, petite /pətit/, petits /pəti/, petites /pətit/: the feminine -e makes the t sound, the plural -s is silent	grammar
What happens to the t of petit before a vowel?	It is said: un petit enfant /œ̃ pətit‿ɑ̃fɑ̃/, un petit ami /œ̃ pətit‿ami/; the plural s gives /z/: de petits enfants /də pətiz‿ɑ̃fɑ̃/	sound
Small children and the grandchildren: the same sounds, what differs?	de petits enfants /də pətiz‿ɑ̃fɑ̃/ are small children; les petits-enfants /le pətiz‿ɑ̃fɑ̃/, with the hyphen, are the grandchildren	words
She is short: petit or court?	Elle est petite. /ɛl ɛ pətit/ Of a person petit is short; court is short in length: une jupe courte /yn ʒyp kuʁt/	grammar

Peas: des petits pois or de petits pois?	des petits pois /de pəti pwa/: petit is part of the name, so des stays; for a description careful French writes de: de petits jardins /də pəti ʒaʁdɛ̃/	grammar
