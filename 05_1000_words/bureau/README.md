# un bureau — a desk, an office

## The word

Conventions: on the [chapter page](../README.md).

- *un bureau* /œ̃ byʁo/: two of the alphabet's drills in one word, the /y/ of the letter *u* and the /o/ of *eau*, three letters for one vowel, as in *beau* /bo/, *l'eau* /lo/, *un château* /œ̃ ʃɑto/. The plural takes *-x*, *des bureaux* /de byʁo/, like every noun in *-eau*; Szypowska's [lesson 6](../../03_Szypowska/lecon_06_en_visite/README.md) has the rule. Nothing changes in sound.
- *Un* /œ̃/ says the noun is masculine: *le bureau* /lə byʁo/, *les bureaux* /le byʁo/.
- One word, two things: the desk, *sur mon bureau, il y a mes livres et mes cahiers* /syʁ mɔ̃ byʁo, il j a me livʁ e me kaje/ from Adam's text in [La maison](../../02_Yosser_teacher/lesson_01_la_maison/README.md), and the office, *je vais au bureau* /ʒə vɛ o byʁo/ I am going to the office. The sentence says which; *au bureau* /o byʁo/ is nearly always the office.
- Three offices with a counter: *le bureau de poste* /lə byʁo də pɔst/ the post office, *le bureau de tabac* /lə byʁo də taba/ the tobacconist, which sells stamps and newspapers too, *le bureau de vote* /lə byʁo də vɔt/ the polling station.

## AI section: grammar, phrases, examples, exercises

- **-eau, -eaux.** The plural in *-x*: *un bureau, des bureaux* /byʁo/, *un château, des châteaux* /ʃɑto/, *un cadeau, des cadeaux* /kado/, *un gâteau, des gâteaux* /ɡɑto/, *un oiseau, des oiseaux* /wazo/, *l'eau, les eaux* /o/. The same for *-eu*: *un jeu, des jeux* /ʒø/. The sound never changes; the article does, and the liaison before a vowel, *des oiseaux* /dez‿wazo/. The nouns in *-s, -x, -z* change nothing at all, on the page of [un tapis](../tapis/README.md).
- **On the desk.** *La lampe de bureau* /la lɑ̃p də byʁo/, *la chaise de bureau* /la ʃɛz də byʁo/, *le tiroir* /lə tiʁwaʁ/ the drawer, *l'ordinateur* /lɔʁdinatœʁ/ the computer, *le clavier* /lə klavje/ the keyboard, *l'écran* /lekʁɑ̃/ the screen, *le stylo* /lə stilo/ the pen, *le cahier* /lə kaje/ the notebook, *le livre* /lə livʁ/ the book, *les papiers* /le papje/ the papers, *la corbeille* /la kɔʁbɛj/ the waste-paper basket.
- **At the office.** *Aller au bureau* /ale o byʁo/, *être au bureau* /ɛtʁ o byʁo/, *travailler au bureau* /tʁavaje o byʁo/, *un collègue de bureau* /œ̃ kɔlɛɡ də byʁo/, *le chef de bureau* /lə ʃɛf də byʁo/, *les heures de bureau* /lez‿œʁ də byʁo/ office hours, *le télétravail* /lə teletʁavaj/ working from home, *en télétravail* /ɑ̃ teletʁavaj/.
- **Bureau on the screen.** *Le bureau* /lə byʁo/ is the desktop of a computer as well: *sur le bureau de l'ordinateur* /syʁ lə byʁo də lɔʁdinatœʁ/.
- **Bureau, the word.** From *la bure* /la byʁ/, the coarse cloth that covered the writing table: first the cloth, then the table, then the room, then the people who sit in it, *le bureau de l'association* /lə byʁo də lasɔsjasjɔ̃/ the committee.
