# Lesson 4: Chez les Lefèvre

## The rules of the lesson

- **The letter c** is /s/ before *i* (*ici, voici*), /k/ before *a* and *u* (*cabinet, cuisine*), and /k/ at the end of a short word after a vowel (*Bac, avec*).
- **Elision:** *le* loses its vowel before a vowel: *l'appartement*.
- **The genitive, continued.** *de* alone before a proper name (*de Jean, de Marie, de Monsieur Lefèvre*); *du*, contracted from *de* + *le*, before a masculine noun beginning with a consonant (*du garçon*); *de la* before a feminine noun beginning with a consonant (*de la famille*); *de l'* before any noun, of either gender, beginning with a vowel or a mute *h* (*de l'amie*); *des*, contracted from *de* + *les*, before a plural noun (*des parents*).
- **Realia.** The rue du Bac, literally Ferry Street, runs towards the Seine, to the place where a ferry once crossed and a bridge now stands.
