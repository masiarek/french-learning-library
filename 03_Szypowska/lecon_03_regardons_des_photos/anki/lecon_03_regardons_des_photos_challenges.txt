The three imperative forms of regarder, with IPA	regarde ! /ʁəɡaʁd/ · regardons ! /ʁəɡaʁdɔ̃/ · regardez ! /ʁəɡaʁde/	grammar
Tu regardes, but regarde ! Why is the -s gone?	The imperative of a first-conjugation verb drops the -s of the tu form (only the first conjugation does).	grammar
The definite article in four forms, with an example of each	le salon · la photo · les photos · l’amie (l’ before a vowel, both genders)	grammar
Which take an article: Paris, Varsovie, Pologne, France, Jean?	Countries do: la Pologne, la France. Cities and first names do not: Paris, Varsovie, Jean.	grammar
Say: Pierre’s photo	la photo de Pierre /la fɔto də pjɛʁ/ (de does the work of the Polish genitive)	say
Say: Marie’s friend (a woman)	l’amie de Marie /lami də maʁi/	say
Say: the capital of Poland	la capitale de la Pologne /la kapital də la pɔlɔɲ/	say
Say: In Warsaw people speak Polish.	À Varsovie on parle polonais. /a vaʁsɔvi ɔ̃ paʁl pɔlɔnɛ/	say
Say: Look at the Eiffel Tower! (to one person)	Regarde la Tour Eiffel ! /ʁəɡaʁd la tuʁ ɛfɛl/	say
Which is nearer, voici or voilà?	voici: nearer persons or things; voilà: farther ones. In speech the difference is fading.	grammar
Les photos or des photos: what is the difference in Marie et Jean regardent les photos?	les photos: the photos already mentioned; des photos would be any photos.	grammar
Read the book’s bracket: [wuala]	voilà /vwala/ (the book’s wua is /vwa/)	notation
Read the book’s bracket: [żã]	Jean /ʒɑ̃/ (ż is /ʒ/, ã is /ɑ̃/)	notation
Read the book’s bracket: [warsɔwi]	Varsovie /vaʁsɔvi/ (the book’s w is /v/)	notation
Read the book’s bracket: [la pɔlɔń]	la Pologne /la pɔlɔɲ/ (ń is /ɲ/)	notation
Make plural: Voici la carte de Jean.	Voici les cartes de Jean. /vwasi le kaʁt də ʒɑ̃/	exercise
Make singular: Elles apportent des livres.	Elle apporte un livre. /ɛl apɔʁt œ̃ livʁ/	exercise
Answer no: Aimez-vous parler français ?	Non, nous n’aimons pas parler français. /nɔ̃, nu nɛmɔ̃ pa paʁle fʁɑ̃sɛ/	exercise
Fill in: Tu ___ le Palais ___ ___ Culture.	Tu regardes le Palais de la Culture. /ty ʁəɡaʁd lə palɛ də la kyltyʁ/	exercise
Translate: Here are three postcards and four photos.	Voici trois cartes et quatre photos. /vwasi tʁwa kaʁt e katʁ fɔto/	exercise
Where is the liaison in nous aimons, and what sound does it add?	nous‿aimons /nuz‿ɛmɔ̃/: the silent s of nous sounds as /z/ before the vowel.	sound
