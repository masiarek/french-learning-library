# Lesson 3: Regardons des photos

## The rules of the lesson

- **voici, voilà.** In principle *voici* points at persons or things nearer, *voilà* at farther ones; in everyday speech the difference is fading.
- **The imperative (impératif)** is the verb without its subject pronoun: *regarde !, regardons !, regardez !* The *tu* form drops the final *-s* of *tu regardes*, but only in the first conjugation.
- **The definite article:** *le* masculine, *la* feminine, *les* plural for both genders, *l'* before a vowel in both genders (like German *der, die* and English *the*). It is used for persons and things already known or mentioned (*les photos*, as against *des photos*, any photos), for a noun made definite by *de* (*la photo de Pierre*), for things unique of their kind (*le Palais de la Culture, la Tour Eiffel*) and with names of countries (*la Pologne, la France*). First names, surnames and city names take no article.
- **The genitive.** French nouns do not decline; *de* does the work of the Polish second case: *la photo de Suzanne, l'amie de Marie, la capitale de la Pologne.*
- **The preposition à** answers *where?* with the name of a city: *à Paris, à Varsovie* (Polish *w*).
