# Lesson 3: Regardons des photos

**Level:** 101 · for a Polish reader working through the third lesson of Szypowska's course

**One line:** The whole of lesson 3, pages 13 to 17 of the book, as one table: the reading *Regardons des photos* with the pictures of Suzanne and Pierre, Paris and Warsaw; the word list and the *Zwroty*; every French word and phrase of the grammar pages, which teach the imperative, the definite article, *de* as the genitive and *à* with a city; and the five exercises, the last of them a translation into French. A program types in the book's own bracketed transcriptions, reletters them with the [chapter's key](../README.md) and holds them to the page's IPA: 29 brackets, and the only disagreements are the final vowel of *photo* and the first vowel of *Eiffel*.

## What the lesson is

Szypowska's lessons have a fixed shape. A reading or dialogue comes first, with the book's phonetic transcription in square brackets under every line and a drawing beside it. Then *Słowniczek*, the word list with Polish glosses, and *Zwroty*, the phrases. Then *Objaśnienia*, notes on pronunciation and on realia (here the Eiffel Tower: built by the engineer Eiffel for the World's Fair of 1889, 306 m high by the book's figure, lit at night, a beacon turning at the top, a radio and television station inside). Then *Wiadomości gramatyczne*, the grammar, in Polish with French examples. Then *Ćwiczenia*, the exercises. This page keeps the French of all of it in one table and leaves the Polish prose out, as the owner asked.

## How to read the table

- **French** is the text as printed. Bold rows are the sections of the lesson. A blank in an exercise is `________`; an answer of mine follows the book's sentence after →, or sits in square brackets inside the IPA where the sentence has a blank.
- **English, with the Polish below it.** In the reading the Polish is mine. In the word list, the *Zwroty* and the grammar examples it is the book's, kept as printed; *(ż)* after *l'amie* is the book's gender mark, feminine. In the exercises the answers and translations are mine, and the Polish of exercise 5 is the book's sentence to translate.
- **IPA** follows Le Robert's key, written from memory, as in [English and French in the IPA](../../01_Sounds/ipa_english_vs_french/README.md). ‿ marks a compulsory liaison (*nous‿aimons* /nuz‿ɛmɔ̃/). The book's own brackets are not repeated in the table; the program below holds them.

## What the Polish pages say, in short

- **voici, voilà.** In principle *voici* points at persons or things nearer, *voilà* at farther ones; in everyday speech the difference is fading.
- **The imperative (impératif)** is the verb without its subject pronoun: *regarde !, regardons !, regardez !* The *tu* form drops the final *-s* of *tu regardes*, but only in the first conjugation.
- **The definite article:** *le* masculine, *la* feminine, *les* plural for both genders, *l'* before a vowel in both genders (like German *der, die* and English *the*). It is used for persons and things already known or mentioned (*les photos*, as against *des photos*, any photos), for a noun made definite by *de* (*la photo de Pierre*), for things unique of their kind (*le Palais de la Culture, la Tour Eiffel*) and with names of countries (*la Pologne, la France*). First names, surnames and city names take no article.
- **The genitive.** French nouns do not decline; *de* does the work of the Polish second case: *la photo de Suzanne, l'amie de Marie, la capitale de la Pologne.*
- **The preposition à** answers *where?* with the name of a city: *à Paris, à Varsovie* (Polish *w*).

## Po polsku, w skrócie

Cała trzecia lekcja podręcznika Szypowskiej w jednej tabeli: czytanka „Regardons des photos", słówka i zwroty, francuskie przykłady z objaśnień i z gramatyki (tryb rozkazujący, rodzajnik określony, „de" jako dopełniacz, „à" przed nazwą miasta) oraz pięć ćwiczeń, z których ostatnie to tłumaczenie na francuski. Polskie objaśnienia nie są przepisane; po angielsku jest tłumaczenie, a pod nim polski, w słówkach ten z książki.

Podręcznik zapisuje wymowę własnym systemem w nawiasach kwadratowych.
